# Playbook: Executive Communication

MBA source: MGMT6501Q Communication for Business Leaders.  
Attach whenever the audience is HQ, board, C-suite, or a difficult 1:1 / meeting.

**Load first:** `mba-notes/MGMT6501Q/cheat-sheet.md` (three-question prep, Munter strategy, feedback ABCD, crisis/Tylenol framework) → `frameworks.md` for templates → `career-hooks.md` for HK healthcare/pharma examples.

---

## Before writing

1. Audience and power dynamic  
2. Channel: one-to-many / one-to-one / meeting  
3. Success measure (decision? alignment? information only?)  
4. Sensitivity tier — strip High detail from forwardable drafts  

## Output patterns

### A. Upward brief (email or slide bullets)

```markdown
## Ask (one sentence)
## Why now
## Recommendation
## Options considered (2–3 lines)
## Risks / asks of HQ
## Decision needed by (date)
```

### B. Difficult one-to-one

```markdown
## Purpose of the conversation
## Facts (shared)
## My position
## Their likely concerns
## Questions to ask (not accusations)
## Desired outcome + fallback
```

### C. Meeting + impromptu Q&A

```markdown
## Meeting objective (one line)
## Agenda (3 bullets max)
## Decision requested
## Anticipated hard questions + 2-sentence answers
## Parking lot items (explicitly out of scope today)
```

## Quality bar

- [ ] Ask / recommendation appears in the first 5 lines  
- [ ] No unexplained jargon  
- [ ] High-sensitivity detail removed from shareable version  
- [ ] Clear owner and date for next step
