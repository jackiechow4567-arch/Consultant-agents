## HK subsidiary-specific

- **Reimbursement reality:** US $370K orphan logic rarely applies — model **HA Drug Formulary, SSF, private pay, employer** paths explicitly.  
- **Digital health bridge:** 59% wearable users discuss data with HCPs — PM/GM should plan **HCP workflow + liability**, not consumer app alone.  
- **China biotech BD:** rising innovator pipeline — geopolitics + data/export controls in partner diligence ([Agent 05 tech-geopolitics](../../agents/05-tech-geopolitics.md) when live).  
- **Pear Therapeutics lesson (syllabus reading):** digital therapeutics **trailblazer tax** — pioneer without reimbursement = cautionary tale for HK digital formularies.  
- **Non-therapeutic proliferation:** faster ROI in wellness/devices — GM must guard **brand/clinical claim boundary** vs regulated therapeutics core.
