## Healthcare innovation paradoxes (Session 1)

| Paradox | Mechanism | Agent implication |
|---------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Valley of death** | ~1 in 1,000 leads → trial; ~1 in 10 FIH → approval; ~$2.6B / 12+ years | Don't treat preclinical assets like near-term revenue |
| **Affordability** | Median new FDA drug ~$370K; orphan share rising; prices doubled vs 2021 | HK access/reimbursement is binding — not US list price |
| **Asia representation** | ~6% Asian trial patients vs ~60% global population; disease burden skewed to Asia | Local evidence gap for stomach, liver, NPC, NSCLC in non-smokers |
| **AMR** | Low volume + low price + reserve-use → pharma exit; 26 new classes 1900–1990 vs 1 since | Structural misalignment — not just "more innovation" |
| **Better at finding than treating** | Diagnostics/digital proliferate; therapeutics slower | Corporate innovation portfolio — balance screen vs treat |
| **COVID lesson** | Best biotech + funding ≠ population outcome without ecosystem coordination | Weak-link analysis for any HK launch (clinical + access + comms) |

## Regulatory pathway spectrum (time / capital)

| Tier | Examples | Typical timeline | Capital intensity |
|------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **High-reg life sciences** | NCE, biologics, Phase I–III | 10–12 years | $1B+ |
| **Regulated medtech / SaMD** | Class III PMA, Class II 510(k), SaMD | 1–7 years | Medium–high |
| **Borderline / wellness** | Nutraceuticals, cosmeceuticals | 0.5–2 years | Lower |
| **Non-medical consumer** | Wellness wearables, non-diagnostic apps | 0.5–2 years | Lowest |

**Agent rule:** classify innovation **before** recommending build/partner/invest — regulatory class drives timeline, evidence, and HQ appetite.

## Unique Value Proposition framework (Session 2)

### Part A — The proposition

| Block | Key questions |
|-------|---------------|
| **Scale of opportunity** | Market targeted yet big enough? (wellness, therapeutics, devices, AI, services) |
| **Unmet need ("the pain")** | Disease burden, patient population, severity, current/pipeline alternatives, ethical/access constraints |
| **Your solution** | How it solves the problem; compelling vs alternatives |
| **Moat(s)** | IP, trade secrets, regulatory barrier, data, brand, network |

**Industry analysis tools:** Porter Five Forces, SWOT, competitor benchmarking (direct + indirect).

### Part B — Making it happen

| Block | Key questions |
|-------|---------------|
| **Business model** | Value chain / stakeholder map / canvas — revenue model + unit economics (LTV, CAC) |
| **Execution / milestones** | Base camp → Camp 1/2/3 plateaus; Gantt; critical path (Brooks's law) |
| **Organizational needs** | Internal + **interprise** leverage (CDMO, CRO, JV, licensing, SaaS, incubators) |
| **Risks & mitigants** | Technical, regulatory, commercial, team — "soft stuff is hardest" |
| **Success metrics** | Balanced scorecard — not revenue only |

**CEO time lesson:** unblock others, memo culture, calendar-as-todo, customer empathy — apply to GM driving digital/clinical programs.

**Transformation formula:** Effect = Quality × Acceptance (Q × A) — high-quality clinical/commercial plan fails without change acceptance.

## Investor lens checklist (Session 3)

Before recommending invest / partner / kill:

1. Commercial potential / scale  
2. Technology quality vs unmet need significance  
3. Industry position vs current and future competitors  
4. Barriers to entry / moats  
5. Strategy coherence  
6. Execution challenges — what must go exceptionally well  
7. Major risks  
8. Management team strength

## Venture financing essentials

### Financial model principles
- Modular build; P&L → cash flow; one-source assumptions (color-coded)  
- Key drivers only (80/20); base / optimistic / pessimistic cases  
- Questions answered: burn, breakeven, hiring pace, cash-out date  

### Valuation toolkit

| Method | Use when |
|--------|----------|
| **Comparables (public)** | P/E, EV/Revenue, MAU — watch negative E, imperfect peers |
| **Transaction comps** | M&A multiples by stage (pre-clinical → marketed biopharma deals) |
| **Venture Capital Method** | (1) Terminal/exit value + years (2) Discount at target IRR (3) Post-money = FV/(1+r)^n (4) Ownership % = Investment / post-money |
| **Pre-money** | Post-money − investment |

### Returns
- **MOIC** = exit / invested  
- **IRR** — time-adjusted; use together ("3× in 5 years")  

### Funding stages
Match round to development stage, risk, investor mandate (angel → seed → A/B → growth → IPO/M&A).

## Corporate vs startup innovation (Session 4 / course themes)

| Dimension | Startup / venture | Corporate / subsidiary |
|-----------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Risk appetite | High; portfolio of bets | Constrained by brand, compliance, HQ hurdle rates |
| Speed | Fast pivot; grit + Plan B/C | Slower; governance, RA, Medical, Legal |
| Funding | VC milestones, dilution | Internal capex, BD deals, incubators (HSITP, university tech transfer) |
| Failure mode | Run out of cash | Reputational + regulatory + HQ capital allocation |
| Advantage | Focus, urgency | Distribution, data, regulatory infrastructure, patient access |

**Corporate GM playbook:** use venture **frameworks** for option evaluation; use **corporate gates** for execution (evidence grade, HA/reimbursement, pharmacovigilance).
