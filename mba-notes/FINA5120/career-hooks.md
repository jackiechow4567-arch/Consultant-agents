## HK / PharmaEssentia-specific hooks

- **Subsidiary P&L vs project NPV:** Hitting monthly revenue must not justify negative-NPV compliance shortcuts.  
- **HA formulary:** Multi-quarter strategic project — model as staged investment, not one-shot expense.  
- **PSP:** Treat as pricing/access tool with measurable incremental volume and cost — transparent to HQ (role-gm Don’t).  
- **Cold-chain / distributor:** Working capital (NWC) and logistics SLAs are real CF drivers.  
- **Sensitivity:** Exact P&L, PSP costs → `work-briefs/` only; agent uses ranges externally.
