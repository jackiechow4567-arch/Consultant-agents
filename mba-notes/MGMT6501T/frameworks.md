## 3. Groysberg C-suite skills — selection logic

| Company state | Leadership type often sought |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| New strategy / tipping point | Visionary leadership |
| Performance lift needed | Take-charge / driving higher performance |
| Talent-heavy, knowledge firm | Non-authoritarian, trust-building |
| Turnaround / transformation | Change-agent, external hire |
| Regulated / reputational sensitivity | Ethical leadership, integrity foreground |

```
STRATEGY & OUTLOOK          OPERATIONS & PEOPLE         FINANCE & GOVERNANCE
──────────────────          ───────────────────         ────────────────────
Business model choice       Org & process               Debt / capital structure

Opportunities               Leadership style            CSR
Risks & mitigation          Culture & values            Disclosure / comms
```

**Sequencing heuristic:** stabilize balance sheet and stakeholder confidence → clarify strategy → fund growth → brand/innovation bets.

## 5. Case decision frameworks

### 5a. Joint venture underperformance

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| Diagnose | Market vs asset vs governance vs capital starvation |
| Options | Inject capital, restructure ownership, manage-for-cash, exit |
| Stakeholder map | Local minorities, strategic partner, board, lenders |
| Deal design | Partial buyout, mix-and-match, scrip alternative |
| Commitment | Long-term capex plan with remaining shareholders locked in |

### 5b. Capital-constrained greenfield

| Party | Interest |
|-------|----------|
| Company | Brand control, long-term presence, limited balance-sheet risk |
| Landowner | Area uplift, luxury anchor, building investment |
| Operators | P&L risk they may refuse |

**Structure tools:** split development entity, operating lease/management contract, phased funding, partner bears construction vs operating risk separately.

## 7. CEO ↔ rising leader collaboration

What CEOs look for in C-suite appointees:
- Demonstrated leadership (not just functional excellence)
- Strategic + execution balance
- Team builder, not solo star
- Board-ready communication
- Integrity and judgment under ambiguity
- Willingness to own outcomes (success and failure)

What rising leaders should do:
- Understand CEO's top 3 priorities before proposing initiatives
- Frame requests as decisions with tradeoffs, not problems
- Build cross-functional coalition before board exposure
- Protect CEO/board from surprises — especially compliance and financial
