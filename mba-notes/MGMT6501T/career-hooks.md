## HK subsidiary-specific

- **HQ vs local CEO tension:** You are seldom "CEO" but must **act like one locally** — own P&L narrative, compliance, and stakeholder map while aligning to group standards.  
- **Capital constraints:** Tokyo pattern applies when HQ freezes capex but HK market window is open — propose structured deals (local entity, phased investment, partner co-funding).  
- **Luxury vs mass-market parallel:** Peninsula brand discipline ≈ protecting **premium brand/clinical positioning** vs volume shortcuts in pharma.  
- **INED mindset:** Before group meetings, ask "what would an independent director challenge?"  
- **Post-Covid lesson (course context):** macro shocks (travel, hospital access, tender delays) belong in **risk & mitigation** block of any GM plan.
