## What this course teaches

Practical C-suite lens from a 45-year career (accountancy → investment banking → CFO → 23-year CEO of Peninsula Hotels). Focus: **what CEOs actually do**, how they decide, and how rising leaders collaborate with senior management and boards.

## CEO job facets (Session 1)

| Dimension | What effective CEOs manage |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| Vision & ambition | Clear direction, goals, values, culture |
| Trust & respect | Loyalty, stakeholder relationships |
| Organization & process | Structure, decision rights, governance rhythm |
| Decision-making | Fair judgment, speed vs quality tradeoffs |
| Innovation | Creativity, improvement initiatives |
| Execution | Planning, detail, delivery |
| Communication & influence | Up (board/HQ), across (peers), down (teams) |
| Negotiation | Style matched to counterparty and stakes |
| People | Recruitment, training, development, empowerment |
| Crisis & risk | Key risks monitored; dive deep when needed |
| Professional depth | Finance, legal, marketing, HR, technical — enough to challenge |

**Success definition:** not one metric — balance short-term performance, long-term brand/asset value, stakeholder trust, and personal development of the leadership bench.

## Seven C-suite skills (Groysberg, HBR)

| # | Skill | Agent use |
|---|-------|-----------|
| 1 | **Leadership** (type depends on company need: visionary, take-charge, authentic/trust-building) | Match recommendation tone to turnaround vs growth context |
| 2 | **Strategic thinking + execution** ("operating savvy") | One-page strategy must include **how** it gets done |
| 3 | **Technical / technology literacy** | GM must understand digital, finance, regulatory basics — not delegate blindly |
| 4 | **Team & relationship building** | No brilliant lone wolf; build leadership team |
| 5 | **Communication & presentation** | Board-ready, multi-stakeholder, external scrutiny |
| 6 | **Change management** | Continuous improvement mindset; external hires often brought to drive change |
| 7 | **Integrity** | Non-negotiable; speed of communication amplifies ethical risk |

**Trend:** global outlook, team player over star culture, soft skills rising vs pure technical depth.

## New CEO action plan framework (Session 2 — HSH 2002)

When taking a distressed or transition CEO role, structure the first 90-day / 12-month plan:

| Block | Questions |
|-------|-----------|
| **Outlook & strategy** | Owner-operator vs management contracts? Growth where (geo, segment, format)? |
| **Culture & environment** | Leadership style, values, internal climate |
| **Opportunities & growth** | Revenue enhancement (retail, apartments, adjacencies) |
| **Risks & mitigation** | Debt, interest cover, asset write-downs, macro shocks |
| **Management & operations** | Management board process, property-level needs, capex allocation |
| **Stakeholders** | Shareholders, lenders, partners, regulators, staff |
| **People development** | Training, incentives, succession |
| **Financial management** | Deleveraging, capital raising, disposals, cost efficiency (build + run) |
| **Governance & comms** | Disclosure, internal/external messaging, CSR |
| **Brand** | Enhancement vs dilution from expansion choices |

**Distressed-context signals:** low profit, high leverage, weak interest cover, struggling new assets, no committed pipeline — prioritize **survival + credibility** before growth bets.

## CEO decision patterns (Session 3 mini-cases)

### Pattern: distressed JV / minority partners (Manila)
1. Understand asset needs; feasibility on rejuvenation options  
2. Secure board backing for long-term investment  
3. Engage all shareholders on preferences  
4. Structure deal (buy partial stakes, mix-and-match, share alternative)  
5. Lock remaining shareholders into long-term investment commitment  

**GM lesson:** when local partners won't fund R&M, **restructure ownership or exit** — don't subsidize indefinitely.

### Pattern: capital-constrained flagship project (Tokyo)
- Site value high but company cannot fund post-crisis  
- Landowner wants luxury anchor; willing to invest in building, not operating risk  
- CEO preserves **product/design control** while unlocking partner capital  
- Structure separates **asset development** from **operating P&L exposure**

**GM lesson:** creative deal structure beats "deal is dead" — align who bears which risk.

### Pattern: asset renovation (Peak Tower)
- Iconic asset needing repositioning — board-level capex decision with brand implications

## Board / HQ communication checklist

| Audience | Lead with | Avoid |
|----------|-----------|-------|
| **Board** | Decision needed, options, recommendation, risk/mitigation, capital ask | Operational detail without strategic frame |
| **HQ** | Alignment to group strategy, local constraints, explicit tradeoffs | Surprises; unsigned compliance gaps |
| **Direct reports** | Clear priorities, empowerment boundaries, success metrics | Micromanagement without context |

**Simulation prep (Session 4 — syllabus only):** board roles (Chair, CEO, CFO, COO, BD, Brand, Projects, HR, Legal, INEDs); phased decisions on major development project; team reports decisions + actions each phase.
