## Clinical Project Manager

| Course tool | Work application |
|-------------|------------------|
| **Trust equation** | Investigator or CRO call: credibility is protocol fluency; reliability is the follow-up you send the same day; intimacy is one human beat; self-orientation is not using their time to show you are busy. |
| **Airtime / super-facilitator** | Multi-site calls where the US lead and the monitor talk, and the site coordinator never does. Name the quiet role and ask them a specific question. |
| **Edmondson matrix** | A deviation review that is high accountability and low safety produces anxiety and hidden errors. Separate “what happened” from “who is in trouble” before you ask for causes. |
| **Sync vs async** | Lab alerts and safety signals are sync. Status and document chase are async. Do not make a 15-hour time difference carry routine updates. |
| **Meyer — deciding & evaluating** | A blunt US-style deviation critique can land as shame or as unclear, depending on the scale gap. Say whether you are consulting or deciding. |
| **Channel energy** | Back-to-back sponsor/CRO/site calls are Gardner’s channeling problem. One goal for the call. Do not read silence from a home office as low commitment. |

## Product Manager

| Course tool | Work application |
|-------------|------------------|
| **Vertical takeoff + one takeaway** | KOL or HA forum on camera: open with the clinical question, close with one line they can repeat to a colleague. |
| **Hewlett — command of the virtual room** | Launch or advisory board: visuals, who is called on, and a listen-to-learn question beat a dense slide. |
| **Burkus** | Cross-functional brand brainstorm: research the access question first, allow task conflict, do not score ideas before a payer or clinician has touched them. |
| **Galinsky vision** | Access narrative: values-based and concrete (“what the physician can say to the patient”), repeated in medical, market access, and commercial rooms — not three different stories. |
| **Livermore — autonomy** | Asking every affiliate to “just own it” fails when the local team expects a framed decision. Say what is theirs and what stays central. |

## General Manager

| Course tool | Work application |
|-------------|------------------|
| **Aristotle** | Hybrid HK office: colocation will not fix a team that lacks safety, dependability, or a clear goal. Do not spend the culture budget on seating. |
| **Gardner — generate vs multiply** | After a heavy quarter, a town hall that only adds goals channels nothing. If people are empty, co-create one meaningful priority. If they are flat, use the sides of them you have been ignoring. |
| **Montañez** | Affinity distance inside a small HK office that also reports to a distant HQ. Recognition that names the act. Career paths that do not require being in the headquarters time zone. |
| **Trust / self-orientation** | HQ update on camera: their decision is the point. Your effort is not the story. |
| **Wadhwa — disarm + right frame** | A tense affiliate or HA conversation: find the true part of their criticism before you defend the plan. |
| **Four-fold way** | Show up, tell the truth without blame, stay unattached to winning the hour. |

## Time-zone rule of thumb (PDT)

US Pacific 16:00–18:00 = Hong Kong **07:00–09:00 the next calendar day**.  
After the US moves to PST (first Sunday in November), the same Pacific block is **08:00–10:00 HKT**, still the next morning.  
Rotate who takes the ugly hour. Do not call that rotation “flexibility” if the same site always loses.
