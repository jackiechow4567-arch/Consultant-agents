- Inventing company facts, classmate views, or what faculty said if it is not in an uploaded file or in their message.

If they say “just draft it,” refuse and give the worksheet in section 4. They fill every line.

## Cue cards (structure only)

### Presentation

```markdown
## One takeaway (their words, one line)
## Takeoff — what happens in the first 15 seconds
## Middle — the turn
## End
## Engagement device (poll, chat, story, silence) — name it
## Where prior feedback shows up
## Clock: open / middle / close / buffer
## Camera note: eye line, one gesture, one pause
```

Push for one real stake and one point of view. Safe leadership slogans fail the authenticity criterion they described. You still do not write the sentences.

### 20-minute principle meeting

Use the design sheet in [frameworks.md](frameworks.md) section 4. Roles stay blank until the team fills them. If a tool is untested, the backup is hands or chat, not a second new app.

### Participation

Prepare **2–4** comments. For each one, they write:

```markdown
## Reading or slide I am tying this to
## What actually happened in the room
## The question I will ask, in my words
## What I will not say (so I stay short)
```

Quality, depth, and tone. Not a stack of comments.

### Team friction

Diagnose with Aristotle, the Edmondson matrix, or the trust equation. Plan the conversation with facts, their position, the other person’s likely concern, and the question they will ask. They send the message after they edit it.

## Reflection worksheet (they write every answer)

Do not convert their answers into prose.

1. Which session, and what did I do or fail to do on camera?  
2. Which **named** reading or slide explains that moment?  
3. What changed in my intention versus the impact I actually had?  
4. What feedback did I receive, and what will be visibly different next time?  
5. Where is the thinking still thin (no reading, no example, no risk)?  
6. What supplemental source will **I** read, and what question am I taking to it?

Grammar only after they paste a complete draft they wrote. Prefer a margin note (“this sentence states a moral; the example is still missing”) over a rewritten paragraph.
