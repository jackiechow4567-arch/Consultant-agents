## 1. Presence before the first word

```
INTENTION (what I want them to remember / feel / do)
        ?
        ?
FIVE POWERS — all five, not a favorite
Intention · Presence · Communication · Knowledge · Conviction
        ?
        ?
CHANNEL — Words · Voice · Non-verbal
        ?
        ?
IMPACT (what they actually took)
        ?
        ??? gap = the next rep
```

**Setup checklist (supports presence; it is not the performance):**

- [ ] Camera at eye height, lens is the eye line  
- [ ] Light on the face, not behind the head  
- [ ] Frame: head and hands, not a ceiling shot  
- [ ] Audio tested; one voice track  
- [ ] Background quiet enough that you are the subject  
- [ ] Platform move rehearsed (share, chat, poll) so the tool does not steal the takeoff  

**When energy drops:** name it in one sentence, change the participation mode (chat, poll, pair), then return to the purpose. Do not talk louder.

## 2. Virtual story

```
TAKEOFF (question, scene, or fact — then a pause)
    ? BEGINNING (stakes)
    ? MIDDLE (the turn)
    ? END (what changed)
    ? ONE SENTENCE they can repeat
```

Vocal variety is the delivery tool: pace, pitch, pause. “Deep & brief” is on the user’s class capture of this slide; the PDF text layer does not contain those words. Confirm the faculty gloss before you teach it to the team.

**Keep / change / try** after any delivery (use only feedback you were actually given):

| Keep | Change | Try next time |
|------|--------|----------------|
| Behavior that landed | Behavior + impact | One specific experiment |

## 3. Trust on a remote team

```
        Credibility + Reliability + Intimacy
Trust = ----------------------------------------
              Self-Orientation
```

| Lever | On a virtual call |
|-------|-------------------|
| Credibility | You know the matter. You say what you do not know. |
| Reliability | You do the small thing you promised before the next call. |
| Intimacy | One true, bounded personal beat. Not a performance of vulnerability. |
| Self-orientation | You ask, then you stop talking. Their outcome is the point. |

Slide attribution: “Maister, Green & Gelford.” Book spelling is Galford. Confirm.

## 4. Inclusive meeting — design sheet

Use this before a 20-minute team session. Fill it in your own words.

```markdown
## Principle (Haas)
## Why this room, why now (one sentence)
## Takeaway they can say back
## Feeling we are aiming for
## Inclusion devices (name the actual tool)
## Tech setup and the backup if the tool fails
## Roles: host / chat watcher / time / closer
## Part 1 takeoff (0–2)
## Part 2 warm-up (prompt everyone can answer)
## Part 3 exercise (the choice they make)
## Part 4 honorable close (one commitment)
```

**Airtime rule (Zaki / Woolley):** if you can imagine a clock on each person, the facilitator’s clock should not be the longest. Seed a slow start within about ten seconds with your own example, then hand it back. Silence is data. Do not fill all of it.

**Reading the room — change one thing:**

| Signal | Likely read | Move |
|--------|-------------|------|
| Cameras off, chat dead | Safety or tool friction | Written prompt, anonymous if the stake is high |
| Same two voices | Distribution failure | “I want to hear from someone who has not spoken” |
| Fast agreement | Cohesion masking a weak idea (Burkus) | Ask for the cost of saying yes |
| Flat faces after a joke | Context mismatch | Drop the bit; restate the purpose in plain words |
| Chat lively, mics quiet | People will engage if they need not perform | Harvest chat out loud, with names |

**Edmondson matrix — where is this meeting?**

- Anxiety (high bar, low safety): shrink the personal risk of the first answer.  
- Comfort (safe, no bar): add a decision or a commitment.  
- Apathy: the purpose is not theirs. Renegotiate the why.  
- Learning: protect it. Do not add surveillance to “raise energy.”

**Quotidian meeting** (user class capture; not in the three uploaded PDFs — confirm the session):

1. Warm-up  
2. Looking back / reflection  
3. Looking forward / goals  
4. Appreciations + acknowledgments + apologies  
5. Words of inspiration  

Use when the job is the relationship, not a decision. Do not bolt it onto a 20-minute principle meeting.

## 5. Team agreements

```markdown
## Cadence (which hours are legal for whom)
## Sync vs async (what never waits for a call)
## Decision rule (who decides when we disagree)
## Dissent (how we question a rule without paying for it)
## Time zones (whose morning, whose night, and the rotation)
## Response-time norm
## Cameras / chat / language
## How we repair a missed commitment
```

Aristotle order of operations: safety, then dependability, then clarity, then meaning, then impact. A RACI chart does not create safety.

Lencioni: if people will not fight about ideas, look down the pyramid at trust before you demand accountability.

## 6. Cross-cultural call prep

Pick the **one or two scales** that will actually bite. Do not brief the team on all eight.

| If the friction is… | Look at… | Design choice |
|---------------------|----------|----------------|
| “They never put it in writing” / “they over-explain” | Communicating | Decide what must be explicit in the chat |
| Feedback landed as rude or as vague | Evaluating | Match frankness to the relationship, and separate it from how direct the task talk is |
| Your deck did not persuade | Persuading | Principles-first vs applications-first; holistic vs specific |
| People wait for the senior voice | Leading | Say who is allowed to speak first |
| Slow close or a surprise boss decision | Deciding | Tell them whether this meeting decides or consults |
| No trust without dinner, or no trust without delivery | Trusting | One relationship beat **and** one reliable deliverable |
| Silence you read as agreement | Disagreeing | Ask for the objection in a form that is safe here |
| Late start or a rigid agenda | Scheduling | Publish the clock rule |

**Livermore checks before you copy a US leadership habit:**

- Autonomy: is self-direction motivating here, or does it feel like abandonment?  
- Psychological safety: Edmondson’s version may not transfer by slogan. Ask what “speaking up” costs in this hierarchy.  
- Differences: naming culture constantly can freeze people in a stereotype. Meyer: the map is relative, and you may not match your passport.  
- Transparency: owning every mistake in public is not universally read as strength. Expand the repertoire; do not drop honesty.

**Sync vs async:**

| Sync | Async |
|------|--------|
| Dissent, repair, a first relationship, a live read of the room | Status, pre-read, a decision that is already framed, time zones that make a humane hour impossible |

## 7. Energy and loneliness — which problem is it?

```
LOW ENERGY
   ?? scattered across too many calls?     ? CHANNEL (one goal, stop micromanaging inputs)
   ?? depleted, nothing left?              ? GENERATE (meaning they help write; dignity; a win this week)
   ?? capable, unused, flat?               ? MULTIPLY (your real quirk; their unused side)
```

Do not “generate” when the problem is channeling. A vision speech on top of a 252% meeting load (the Microsoft figure Gardner cites) is another meeting.

**Montañez, affinity distance:** community is a feeling and a set of relationships, not a Slack channel. Respect time. Recognition has three beats: what they did, what you experienced, impact. Career support that only happens in a headquarters office recreates physical distance.

## 8. Leadership-in-flow — prep for one event (Wadhwa)

```
Name the event
    ? positive intention
    ? one or two energies
    ? one or two actions you will actually take
    ? after: what energy was in the room, what you did, what changed
```

| Energy | Actions (names only) |
|--------|----------------------|
| **Purpose** | Appeal to values · reaffirm and reexpress · hero’s journey · push / pull / pause / pivot |
| **Wisdom** | Understand before you act · disarm · fuse opposing views · dial an emotion · direct emotional energy · untwist thinking · right frame |
| **Growth** | Growth mindset · solicit advice · acknowledge, apologize, address · learn from adversity · anticipate, assess, adjust |
| **Love** | Appreciate · affiliate · deepen connection · empathize · act/express thoughtfully |
| **Self-realization** | Get centered · affirm a core identity · cultivate intuition · spark joy |

It fails if the feeling is fake. Pick an action whose pathway you already have.

## 9. Inspire and be present (Galinsky + Hewlett)

**Galinsky roles:** visionary (big picture, values, optimistic, concrete image, said more than once) · exemplar (calm, courageous) · mentor (share the lead; do not ignore, diminish, or control).

Monthly loop he recommends: recall when you were the opposite of inspiring · recall someone who was · set one intention · practice in an ordinary interaction.

**Hewlett, what changed inside the old three buckets:**

| Bucket | Still true | Now also required |
|--------|------------|-------------------|
| Gravitas | Confidence, decisiveness | Inclusiveness; respect that shows up in who gets heard |
| Communication | Forcefulness has a place | Command of the virtual room; listen in order to learn |
| Appearance | Polish still reads | Authenticity; the same person online and in the room |

You do not have to max every trait. People who matched the old mold are not finished either.

## 10. Creativity on a virtual whiteboard (Burkus)

```
Research the real question
    ? diverge (this is the only part most “brainstorms” do)
    ? debate the ideas while they are still ideas (task conflict, not personal)
    ? converge
    ? test / prototype
    ? only then pitch for approval
```

Killing moves that look kind: endless sticky notes, a team that always agrees, a leader who scores the idea before any evidence.

## 11. When not to use the full kit

| Situation | Use | Skip |
|-----------|-----|------|
| 15-minute status | Purpose + decision + owner | Quotidian meeting, magical question, five energies tour |
| Safety or regulatory call | Tell, then consult | Open dissent theater before the facts are shared |
| First meeting across a trust gap | One relationship beat + one reliable next step | Full transparency confession |
| Graded reflection or speech | Your own sentences in Rumi | Any paragraph from these notes |
