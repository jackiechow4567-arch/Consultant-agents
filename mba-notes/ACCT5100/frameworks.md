# ACCT5100 — Frameworks reference

> Deeper structure for Finance agent. Prefer [cheat-sheet.md](cheat-sheet.md) for quick reads.

---

## 1. Statement reading workflow

```text
1. Skim auditor opinion + going-concern / emphasis notes
2. Common-size IS and BS (vs prior year + peer)
3. Trace NI → CFO (indirect SCF) — quality of earnings
4. Check investing CF (CapEx intensity) and financing CF (debt/equity)
5. Ratio pack: liquidity, turnover, leverage, ROA/ROE + DuPont
6. Adjust known GAAP differences before concluding
7. Scan contingencies, related parties, subsequent events
8. ESG narrative vs numbers (if relevant) — separate claim from evidence
```

---

## 2. Accrual vs cash quality

| Signal | Interpretation |
|--------|----------------|
| NI ↑ but CFO weak | Accruals heavy — investigate WC, revenue timing |
| CFO ≫ NI | Conservative accruals or one-offs — check |
| Persistent NI without CFO | Red flag for earnings quality |

---

## 3. DuPont / ROE tree

Break ROE into operating efficiency, asset use, and leverage (and interest burden where taught).  
**Use:** Explain *why* ROE moved — not only the headline %.  
**Don't:** Chase ROE via leverage without naming financial risk.

---

## 4. Comparability adjustments

| Issue | Typical fix |
|-------|-------------|
| LIFO vs FIFO | Use LIFO reserve (US) to approximate FIFO inventory/COGS |
| Lease capitalization | Treat operating leases consistently across peers |
| IFRS revaluation / impairment reversal | Strip or note when comparing to US GAAP |
| Interest/dividend classification in SCF | Reclassify to common convention |
| One-time impairments / restructuring | Separate “core” vs reported |

---

## 5. Revenue recognition gate (IFRS 15 / ASC 606)

Before trusting revenue growth:

1. Contract existence  
2. Distinct performance obligations  
3. Transaction price (variable consideration?)  
4. Allocation  
5. Control transferred  

**Pharma/device hook:** Milestone, license, and service bundles need obligation mapping — don't treat cash collection as revenue.

---

## 6. Asset & liability red-flag lenses

| Area | Watch |
|------|-------|
| A/R | Days sales outstanding rising vs sales |
| Inventory | Days on hand rising; LIFO liquidation |
| PPE | Stretching useful lives; delayed impairment |
| Goodwill | Big acquisitions + no impairments for years |
| Contingencies | Vague wording; sudden large provisions |
| Bonds / leases | Off-balance or footnote leverage |

---

## 7. Contracting & incentives lens

Accounting numbers appear in **debt covenants** and **bonus contracts** → managers may manage earnings near thresholds.  
Agent: ask “what contract or target creates pressure here?” before concluding fraud.

---

## 8. ESG overlay

- Identify **scope** of emissions / claims  
- Map to standards/regulation named in filings  
- Separate: measured KPI vs narrative ambition  
- Green bonds / sustainability-linked debt: read covenants and KPIs carefully  

---

## When NOT to over-apply

- Don't restate books without source filings  
- Don't treat common-size alone as valuation  
- Don't ignore auditor / regulatory context  
- Don't give tax or audit opinions
