## HK / PharmaEssentia-specific hooks

- **Subsidiary reporting to HQ:** Common-size + CFO quality beats raw revenue slides.  
- **PSP costs:** Track as transparent expense/CF — don't bury in “sales incentives” (role-gm).  
- **Distributor / cold-chain:** Inventory days and write-downs are early warning.  
- **Sensitivity:** Exact P&L and PSP costs stay in `work-briefs/`; agent uses ratios/ranges externally.  
- **Pair with FINA5120:** Statement items → FCFF for formulary / investment cases.
