## Product Manager

| MGMT5410 tool | Work application |
|---------------|------------------|
| Strategy Triangle | Align local product scope with HQ **what/for whom/where** |
| Strategic fit | Say no to features/channels that are **peripheral** distractions |

## General Manager

## HK subsidiary-specific

- **Scope tension:** HQ global scope vs HK **“we won’t do”** list for local P&L.  
- **Industry analysis:** treat **Hospital Authority / private chains / cross-border** as different Five Forces pictures.  
- **Market entry:** bowling-alley logic for **one hospital system / one specialty** before broad rollout.  
- **Good vs bad competitor:** avoid price wars that shrink category profit pool in regulated markets.
