## 3. Responsible leadership pillars — decision checklist

Before recommending a GM action, score 1–5:

- [ ] Inclusive process — dissent heard early?  
- [ ] Long-term trust protected vs short-term win?  
- [ ] Integrity — would we defend this publicly?  
- [ ] Accountability — who owns outcome?  
- [ ] Stakeholders mapped — who gains/loses?  
- [ ] Empowerment — team capacity built, not hero dependency?

## 4. Behavioral ethics — intervention levers

| Lever | Example |

| **Framing** | Explicit "ethical" checkpoint in launch gate reviews |

| **Accountability** | Named owner for compliance sign-off |

## 6. Conflict typology

```
DISAGREEMENT (low conflict risk)          CONFLICT (intervention may be needed)
──────────────────────────────          ─────────────────────────────────────
Lunch preference                          High-stakes strategy with interdependence
Minor factual dispute                     Both sides claim evidence superiority
Low outcome importance                    Bad Blood–style senior team dysfunction
```

## 7. Naïve realism cycle

```
I am objective → Others should agree → They disagree →
They must be ignorant/biased/evil → Escalation → Conflict
```

**Break cycle:** receptiveness, seek understanding goal, HEAR language, separate people from problem.

## 9. Organizational culture — environment layers

```
PHYSICAL SPACE · AIR · NOISE
         │
    PEERS · SUPPORT STAFF
         │
    NORMS · VALUES IN USE (not just poster)
         │
    LEADERSHIP BEHAVIOR · INCENTIVES
```
