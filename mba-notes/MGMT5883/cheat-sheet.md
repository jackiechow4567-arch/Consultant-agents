|-----------|----------------|--------------------|
| Componential theory | Amabile 1998 + Session 1 | Expertise × creative-thinking skills × motivation; intrinsic motivation is the lever managers can move fastest |

| Creative realism | Thompson Ch.9 | Novel **and** connected to current structures. Fluency, flexibility, originality |
| Divergent vs convergent | Thompson Ch.9 + Session 3 | Individuals diverge better; teams converge (judge) better. Generate apart, evaluate together |
| Brainstorming liabilities | Thompson Ch.9 | Social loafing, conformity / evaluation apprehension, production blocking, performance matching. Nominal groups beat face-to-face on idea count |
| Creative abrasion | Session 3 (Leonard & Swap; Hirshberg) | Style clash is raw material if it stays on the task |

## Case decision snapshots

Scaffolding only. Full maps: [cases.md](cases.md).

**Nest Fresh (Aug 2005, Cyd Szymanski).** Cage-free brand, ~$5.3–5.6M sales, Denver strength (75% of local cage-free), margin squeeze from large caged producers entering specialty. Options on the table: (1) stay independent and push the cage-free coalition / Certified Humane, (2) join a large caged producer with real autonomy, (3) change the cost structure (breakers, density, enriched cages that may forfeit the "cage-free" label). Lecture cue: values define success and fuel intrinsic motivation; setting them aside is a mistake; pursuing them blindly is also a mistake; one value to keep is her own well-being.

**Satera / Imatron (early Sep 1999, fictional).** Seven-person RID team, prototype due 1 Apr 2000, penalty clause for late delivery. Support-structure stall: Bennett's light spoke/wheel twists on the vibration table; Lovas's heavier honeycomb holds. Team leans honeycomb. Pinto has not decided. (B) KAI chart: Ira is the extreme adaptor, David the extreme innovator, Gary moderately above the population mean. Session 4 role play: Gary has chosen Ira's design and must get both engineers to lighten it and finish the mechanical prototype. Slide 5 draws an unlabeled arrow from "cognitive style difference" to "lower team effectiveness."

## Executive application (one line)

Creativity work in a subsidiary is a portfolio choice: protect exploration (time, intelligent failure, discovery behaviors) and keep a delivery path (shared goal, fact-based task conflict, someone who will ship). See [career-hooks.md](career-hooks.md).
