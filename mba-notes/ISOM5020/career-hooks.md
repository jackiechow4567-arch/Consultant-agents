## HK subsidiary-specific

- **Pharma ERP/CRM:** Global SAP/VEEVA rollouts — local regulatory fields and HA reporting need **federal** governance, not pure HQ monarchy.  
- **Hospital access data:** CRM analytics for KOL mapping — data sensitivity drives cloud choice.  
- **Clinical ops hub:** Shared CTMS across brands — feudal BU systems create audit pain.  
- **Digital health bets:** IoT/digital twin pilots — apply RPV: keep innovation unit separate from legacy QA processes.
