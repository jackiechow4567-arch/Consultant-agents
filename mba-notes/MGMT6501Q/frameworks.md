## 1. Communication prep workflow

```
AUDIENCE + CHANNEL + SENSITIVITY TIER
        │
        ▼
Three questions: Remember | Feel | Do
        │
        ▼
Choose style: Tell | Sell | Consult | Join
        │
        ▼
Structure message (Munter: opener → body → close)
        │
        ▼
Deliver efficiently without sacrificing substance
        │
        ▼
Feedback loop: audience answers same three questions
```

---

## 2. Mode × style matrix

| Mode | Typical style | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| One-to-many | Tell / Sell | 90-sec speech, HQ deck |
| One-to-one | Consult / Sell | Retention interview, appraisal |
| Many-to-many | Consult / Join | Crisis team meeting |
| Many-to-many external | Tell + Q&A | Press conference |

---

## 3. Feedback ABCD — giving workflow

```
Observe behavior (facts)
        │
        ▼
Impact on others (remember / feel / do)
        │
        ▼
Constructive suggestion (future-oriented)
        │
        ▼
Direct, balanced, acceptable tone
```

---

## 4. Crisis comms — two-round meeting

```
ROUND 1: DECIDE
├── Facts known / unknown
├── Stakeholder map (CEO, legal, finance, medical, ops, PR)
├── Credo / values filter
└── Action list (recall, packaging, investigation)

ROUND 2: MESSAGE
├── Press objectives (remember, feel, do)
├── Spokesperson roster
├── Written statement (≤90 sec read)
└── Q&A briefing (hostile questions + 2-sentence answers)
```

---

## 5. Difficult 1:1 template

```markdown
## Purpose
## Facts (shared)
## My position
## Their likely concerns
## Questions to ask (not accusations)
## Desired outcome + fallback
```

---

## 6. Meeting + Q&A template

```markdown
## Objective (one line)
## Agenda (≤3 bullets)
## Decision requested
## Hard questions + 2-sentence answers
## Parking lot (explicitly out of scope)
```

---

## 7. When NOT to over-engineer

| Situation | Skip |
|-----------|------|
| Routine FYI | Full consult/join process |
| Time-critical safety | Short tell + follow-up |
| High sensitivity | Forwardable draft without detail |
