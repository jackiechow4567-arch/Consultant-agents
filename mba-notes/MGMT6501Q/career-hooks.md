## HK subsidiary-specific

- **Pharma recall / label change:** J&J-style multi-function crisis team maps to **regulatory + medical + comms + legal** — prepare Q&A before media.  
- **HA engagement:** Brief HA with **substantial + consequential** structure — not data dump.  
- **Returnee vs local team:** Appraisal role-play lessons — credibility and listening before performance judgment.  
- **Parent HQ:** English upward brief; three-question filter prevents 15-slide context before the ask.
