## Core logic (always start here)

**Marketing strategy = two answers:**

1. **Who are our customers?** (target market)  
2. **What value are we providing?** (value proposition)

Then translate strategy → **4Ps tactics**: Product, Price, Distribution, Communication.

```
5-C → Market definition → Segmentation → Targeting → Value prop & Positioning → 4Ps
```

## STP checklist

### Segmentation (useful segments)

- **Homogeneous within** / **heterogeneous across** groups  
- Variables: geo, demo, psycho, lifestyle, usage, decision process  
- Prefer **value-based** (benefits sought, occasion, motivation)  
- Build **segment profile** + **persona** → link to observable traits for targeting  

### Targeting approaches

| Approach | When |
|----------|------|
| Mass / undifferentiated | Rare today; scale play on large need |
| Multitarget / differentiated | Resources to serve multiple segments with distinct offers |
| Niche | Focus one segment deeply (e.g. BMW “love to drive”) |

### Segment evaluation (M-S-D-A-D)

- **M**easurable · **S**ubstantial · **A**ccessible · **D**ifferentiable · **A**ctionable  

### Target choice matrix

Plot **segment attractiveness** (size, growth, profitability, intensity) vs **firm competitive strength**. Bubble size = segment size. Avoid “shot-in-the-dark” (value segment without observable profile).

## Positioning (Session 3)

**Statement template:**

> For **[target market]**, **[Brand X]** is the brand of **[frame of reference]** that **[unique value / POD]** because **[reason to believe]**.

| Element | Role |
|---------|------|
| Target market | Who you serve |
| Frame of reference (FOR) | Category / need / competitor set — sets expectations |
| POD | Point of difference — what you’re known for |
| Reason to believe | Evidence: data, demo, endorsement, proof |

**POP vs POD** — both relative to the chosen FOR.

**Value types:** economic · functional · psychological.

**3R (strong value proposition):** **R**elevance · **R**esonance · **R**ealism.

**Perceptual map** — consumer perception is the ultimate positioning.

**Differentiation when functional parity:** Functional (specs) · **Hedonic** (design/fun — e.g. Renova black TP) · **Values-based** (mission/sustainability — e.g. Patagonia). Commodity traps → add emotional or social meaning.

**Blue Ocean / value innovation:** Redefine competition — eliminate/reduce/raise/create on industry factors (Four Actions). Casella/Yellow Tail: simplified wine for beer drinkers, not better wine for wine snobs.

**Job to be done (benefits):** Functional (“stick things together”) · Social (“look cool to friends”) · Emotional (“feel like a good parent”). If case mentions commoditization → shift benefit level.

## Pricing (Session 5 + EVC)

### CORE framework

| Letter | Lens |
|--------|------|
| **C**ost | Floor — FC:VC ratio, scale, cost vs rivals |
| **O**bjective | Profit / demand / competition / brand signal |
| **R**ivals | Action, position, market structure, strategic intent |
| **E**xperienced value | Ceiling — WTP, elasticity, psychology, fairness |

**Rule:** Cost sets floor; **customer experienced value sets ceiling**. Compete on perceived value, not price cuts alone.

**Profit driver sensitivity:** 1% price change often beats equivalent volume or VC change (~3.3× impact factor in course framing). Price is the highest-leverage profit lever — justify with CORE, don't cut reflexively.

**Skimming vs penetration:** Skimming = high price, lower volume (distinctive/premium). Penetration = low price, drive adoption/habit. Link choice to objective + EVC ceiling.

### Common strategy buckets

Cost-driven · Value/demand-driven · Competition-driven · Strategic positioning · Relationship/retention.

### EVC (Economic Value to Customer)

- **Max price** customer *should* pay vs reference product (lifecycle / cost of ownership)  
- **Relative** — differs by customer usage pattern → **segments + targeting**  
- Charge **below** EVC to give switching incentive  
- Economic benefit alone may fail — need RTB on durability, availability, non-economic value  

### Elasticity

`|ΔQ/Q| / |ΔP/P|` — inelastic <1 · elastic >1.  
Sensitivity drivers: substitutes, comparability, % of wallet, switching cost, stockpiling.

## Product & portfolio (Session 4)

- Product **operationalizes** value prop; success = right **competitive logic** (positioning).  
- **Horizontal** competition (taste/identity) vs **vertical** (rankable superiority).  
- Portfolio: customer logic, product roles (gateway, halo, growth engine), BCG, Ansoff (penetration / development / product / diversification).  

### Brand essentials (Session 4 reading)

- **Brand = meaning in the consumer's mind**, not just name/logo (Landor).  
- **Tangible associations** (performance, taste, durability) vs **intangible** (status, identity, lifestyle) — intangibles harder to copy.  
- **Intended identity ≠ received image** — gap kills brands (Snapple/Quaker lesson). Analyze consumer perception first.  
- **Brand personality types:** Sincere · Exciting · Competent · Sophisticated · Rugged.  
- **Keller resonance pyramid:** Salience → Functional associations (quality judgments) + Psychological associations (feelings) → **Resonance** (loyalty/routine).  
- **Brand equity** = price premium + cost advantages from brand strength; manage every touchpoint.

## Distribution & channels

| Concept | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **Push** | Pay/trade incentives to get retailers to stock (manufacturer → channel) |
| **Pull** | Advertise to consumers so they ask retailers (demand-driven stocking) |
| **Channel conflict** | Direct vs retail; big-box vs specialty — protect dealer margin if that's the model |
| **Place tactic** | Go where **target** is, not where volume is (specialty vs mass) |

## Communication (Session 6)

Plan structure: **Strategy** (goal, benchmarks) → **Message** (audience, message) → **Tactics** (media, creative) → **Implementation** (deployment, development) → **Control** (performance, environment).

Funnel: **Attention → Interest → Desire → Action** — match goal to stage.

**Message ≠ tagline** — message is strategic belief to create; tagline is executional.

**SUCCES (creative stickiness):** **S**imple · **U**nexpected · **C**oncrete · **C**redible · **E**motional · **S**tories.

**Paid / owned / earned media:** Paid = buy reach · Owned = site/email/app · Earned = press/WOM/UGC. Mix by funnel stage.

**Digital tactics (select by goal):** SEM (brand vs generic keywords) · retargeting (warm traffic) · email (retention/ROI) · content marketing (trust before sell) · social by platform.

## Consumer psychology (Session 7)

Marketing funnel maps to internal process:

```
AIDA (marketing)     Attention → Interest → Desire → Action
Psychology (mind)    Attention → Perception → Attitude → Behavior
```

### Attention (gateway)

- **Exposure** (physically present) ≠ **Attention** (noticed) ≠ **Awareness** (remembered later).  
- Drivers: **Sensory salience** (contrast, size, motion) · **Novelty/pleasantness** (humor, surprise) · **Relevance** (cocktail-party effect — personal/goal fit).  
- CAC is rising — relevancy beats bought exposure that doesn't convert.

### Perception (interpretation)

- Perception is **constructive** (expectations fill gaps) and **relative** (context changes judgment).  
- Discount framing vs same price feels different; brand placebo (Red Bull vs identical ingredients).

### Motivation (what drives purchase)

| Driver | Mechanism | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Emotional** | Pride, fear, guilt, excitement | Betty Crocker egg — slight effort reduced guilt |
| **Social** | Informational / normative / value-expressive influence | Opower neighbor comparison; Stanley Cup identity signal |
| **Cognitive** | Goals, efficiency | Goal-gradient effect — pre-stamped loyalty cards fill faster |

### Campaign failure diagnosis

1. Didn't see it? → Fix **attention** (salience, relevance).  
2. Misunderstood it? → Fix **perception** (context, framing, packaging).  
3. Didn't care? → Wrong **motivation** driver (emotional vs social vs cognitive).

## Case analysis protocol (exam-style)

1. **Diagnosis:** Burning platform (financial pain / existential threat / launch opportunity) + 5-C scan + value-trap check (company vs customer definition of value).  
2. **Targeting:** Profitability vs volume — chase **valuable** segment, not biggest. Job-to-be-done benefits.  
3. **Economics:** EVC ceiling for B2B/innovation; CAC vs LTV — spend on high-LTV retention, not low-value awareness.  
4. **Recommendation:** "Target [segment] by positioning [product] as [value prop]" → 4Ps with AIDA stage match.

| Case signal | Likely framework |
|-------------|------------------|
| Saturated market / price wars | Blue Ocean / emotional repositioning |
| Customers don't understand benefits | EVC quantification |
| Limited budget | Sniper niche targeting |
| New generation ignores product | Reposition meaning (De Beers: commitment not marriage) |
| Better specs, flat sales | Psychological funnel — perception gap |

## Marketing arithmetic (must-check numbers)

| Metric | Formula |
|--------|---------|
| Unit contribution | Price − Variable cost |
| Contribution margin % | (Price − VC) / Price |
| Break-even volume | Fixed costs / Unit contribution |
| Profit impact | Unit contribution × Volume − Fixed costs |
| Volume for target profit | (Fixed costs + Target profit) / Unit contribution |
| Required volume after spend | (Fixed + Current profit + Added fixed) / Unit contribution |
| Unit share | Company unit sales / Market unit sales |
| Trade margin | (Selling price − purchase price) / selling price at each channel tier |

**Always stress-test:** Does implied **market share** after the plan pass a realism check? Is **CAC** justified by segment **LTV**?

## Healthcare / HK subsidiary caveats (agent)

- Separate **promotional claims** from **clinical/regulatory evidence** — RTB is often compliance-critical.  
- **FOR choice** shapes which competitors and POP/POD matter (device vs service vs outcome).  
- **EVC** for providers may include workflow, liability, training — not just price.  
- Public vs private HK channels → different segments, elasticity, and procurement logic.  
- HQ global positioning may constrain local FOR/POD — flag tension explicitly.

## 8 questions before recommending (Strategy & Marketing agent)

1. What decision are we making (STP, position, price, product, comm)?  
2. What is the market definition level — and what substitutes are we ignoring?  
3. Who is the economic buyer vs user vs influencer?  
4. What benefits sought (value-based segment)?  
5. What is our POD and RTB — especially for regulated claims?  
6. What is the pricing objective and experienced-value ceiling (EVC)?  
7. Do the numbers work (contribution, break-even, share realism)?  
8. What is sensitive — what must stay out of external tools?

**Don’t:** Framework laundry lists; vague “build brand awareness”; claims without RTB; price cuts without CORE/EVC logic.
