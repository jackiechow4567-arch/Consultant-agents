## HK healthcare-specific hooks



- **FOR choice:** Same product can compete as device, procedure enabler, or outcomes partner — competitors change.  

- **Channels:** Public tender vs private clinic vs KOL-led adoption → different segments, elasticity, and procurement logic.  

- **Claims discipline:** Psychological/economic value props still need **evidence-backed RTB** in regulated categories.  

- **HQ tension:** Global FOR/POD may not fit HK; document local repositioning rationale for HQ.  

- **Commoditized categories:** When specs parity — shift to workflow EVC, service bundle, or values-based differentiation (Patagonia/Renova pattern).  

- **Campaign diagnosis:** Rising digital CAC in HK — test relevance (segment-specific HCP content) before scaling paid reach.  

- **Brand image gap:** Global "innovation leader" intended identity vs HK "unproven locally" received image — fix with local evidence RTB before launch spend.
