## Who I am in this role

- **Title / scope:** PharmaEssentia Hong Kong Product Manager — BESREMi (ropeginterferon alfa-2b), Polycythemia Vera (PV). Post–Oct 2025 HK launch; local commercialization owner (~500 PV patients; HK$36,000/injection list price).
- **Products / domains:** BESREMi for PV only (on-label). Reactive scientific exchange only for investigational ET/MF — no proactive promotion.
- **Core accountabilities:**
  - Post-launch commercialization and access strategy (private → HA Drug Formulary inclusion = #1 milestone)
  - PSP management: Base Case (buy 5 get 1 free); Private Sector (buy 2 get 1 free, private only, first year, expires end 2026) — mapped to patient journey / affordability / onboarding
  - Compliance gatekeeper: all promo/HCP/patient materials vs UMAO (Cap. 231) + HKAPI Code of Practice
  - On-label scientific comms: all PV claims trace to harmonized global lexicon (PROUD-PV/CONTINUATION-PV, Low-PV/NEJM Evidence 2023, PARADIGM Phase 4) — no locally invented claims
  - Reactive exchanges (e.g. ET/MF questions): documented in Veeva; DoH pharmacovigilance/ADR rules
  - Competitive watch: e.g. alternative IFN supply shortages → positioning implications
- **Local vs global:**
  - **Local authority:** pricing execution, PSP ops, HA formulary submission, HK compliance, tactic adaptation
  - **Global constraint:** messaging substance, clinical positioning, lexicon — inherit strictly; zero authority to originate global scientific claims
  - **Evidence:** consume/localize global and regional data (e.g. Taiwan HIDAT); do not generate new global evidence
- **What "good" looks like:**
  - **Primary:** HA Drug Advisory Board + Drug List inclusion — convert private-market base into sustainable public-sector access for ~500 HK PV patients
  - **Secondary:** Steady uptake via Base Case + Private Sector PSPs; **zero** UMAO/HKAPI violations
- **How this differs from my Clinical PM and GM hats:**
  - **PM (commercial, PV/BESREMi):** Decide/recommend local commercialization tactics — pricing execution, PSP rollout (Base Case, Private Sector), HA Formulary submission strategy, promotional compliance (UMAO/HKAPI). Do **not** decide clinical trial protocols, endpoints, or global label/indication scope.
  - **Clinical PM (sponsor trial):** Decide/recommend HK site selection, investigator/site engagement, trial feasibility and enrollment tracking, regulatory/ethics (IRB) timelines, local vendor coordination (labs, CRO) for PharmaEssentia-sponsored trials in HK (ET, PMF, PV lifecycle e.g. PARADIGM). Do **not** decide global protocol design, primary endpoints, SAP, or whether trial data supports label expansion — those sit with global Clinical Development/Biostatistics.
  - **GM (subsidiary lead):** Decide/recommend overall HK P&L trade-offs, budget/headcount allocation across commercial and clinical functions, cross-functional prioritization between trial support and commercial launch. Do **not** decide global pricing corridors, supply allocation, or global R&D portfolio strategy.

## Recurring decisions I face

1. **Formulary evidence packaging and timing** — What pharmacoeconomic/clinical data to prioritize and refine for the next HA Drug Formulary submission window; how to sequence advisory board input to strengthen the dossier.
2. **PSP structure and expiry management** — Monitor uptake of Base Case (buy 5 get 1) vs Private Sector (buy 2 get 1, expiring end 2026); decide whether/how to adjust terms or messaging as the private-sector deadline approaches.
3. **Promotional material and HCP engagement approvals** — Review quarterly campaign/detail-aid content against UMAO/HKAPI compliance while balancing HCP channel mix (advisory boards, private hematologists, academic centers) to grow private-market share ahead of formulary listing.

## Decision criteria / non-negotiables

| Criterion | Weight / rule | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| HA formulary / public access progress | **High** | Strategic north star for the year; can flex on *sequencing* if evidence isn't ready |
| Patient affordability (PSP) | **High** | Bridges private uptake → formulary case; never let PSP term changes compromise compliance or overpromise pricing |
| Evidence / claims compliance (UMAO, HKAPI, global lexicon) | **Hard constraint** | Always wins — any conflict with revenue, timeline, or HQ preference resolves in favor of compliance |
| Private-market uptake / revenue | **Medium** | Sustains commercial credibility and funding; secondary to formulary progress and compliance |
| Effort / team capacity | **Medium** | Sequence so formulary + compliance work always get priority resourcing over incremental promo activity |
| Strategic fit with HQ global brand | **High** | Local tactics must stay within harmonized global lexicon and brand positioning; no local claim deviation even if commercially tempting |
| Timeline (PSP expiry end 2026, formulary window) | **High** | PSP expiry = forcing function for private-to-public transition messaging; formulary window dictates evidence-packaging deadlines |

**Tie-break order:** Compliance → Formulary/Public Access → HQ Strategic Fit → Affordability → Timeline pressure → Revenue → Team Capacity

**Do:**

- Route every new claim or HCP-facing slide through the global lexicon check before local adaptation or translation.
- Log every reactive HCP scientific exchange (including off-label ET/MF questions) through internal MedInfo/compliance channels, citing current local PI.
- Re-confirm PSP eligibility terms (Base Case vs Private Sector) with market access/finance before quoting any HCP or distributor, especially as the 2026 expiry approaches.
- Cap meal hospitality at HKAPI-defined limits — HK$400/attendee for breakfast/lunch, HK$800/attendee for dinner — when sponsoring HCP events or advisory boards.
- Include full abbreviated prescribing information (approved indication, dosage, contraindications, side effects) and the date of approval on every promotional piece, per HKAPI Section 4.
- Ensure any advisory board or symposium agenda dedicates at least two-thirds of the time to genuine scientific content, documented and distributed in advance.

**Don't:**

- Don't extend or modify Private Sector PSP terms without legal/compliance sign-off, even informally with a key opinion leader.
- Don't reference ET or myelofibrosis data in any HK promotional material or proactive HCP conversation — reactive-only; PV-approved indication stays the boundary.
- Don't submit or update HA Formulary evidence packages without first routing through global Medical/Regulatory review, even under submission-window time pressure.
- Don't offer product-branded promotional items (e.g. branded calendars, mouse pads, sticky notes) to HCPs for a prescription-only medicine like BESREMi — explicitly prohibited under HKAPI Section 6.2.
- Don't provide entertainment (theatre, concerts, sporting events) or lavish hospitality at any sponsored event — HKAPI Section 5.3(h) restricts this to modest, meal-incidental activities only.
- Don't engage HCPs as paid consultants/advisors without a signed written contract, documented legitimate need, and fair-market-value compensation set in advance — undocumented "thank you" fees are a Code violation.

## Hard-won lessons (from practice)

1. **Premium vs off-label traditional IFN:** Physicians repeatedly ask why BESREMi costs many times more than traditional interferons used off-label for PV. Without head-to-head data, we cannot claim superior efficacy vs Pegasys or China ropeginterferon — differentiation must stay within approved PV evidence, dosing convenience, and lexicon-approved disease-modifying narrative, not comparative superiority claims.
2. **Competitive noise (Pegasys, China ropeginterferon):** Each physician interaction surfaces "why your drug?" — need a standing, compliant response framework for price/value questions that does not imply unsupported comparative outcomes.

## Frameworks I actually use as PM

**From MBA** (via agent cheat-sheets): MARK5120 (STP/positioning), MGMT5410 (strategic fit), ECON5110 (pricing/rival reaction)

**From practice:**

| Framework | Use for BESREMi PM |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Global harmonized lexicon / PI as claim boundary | **Daily, non-negotiable** — every claim, slide, and HCP conversation checked against this first |
| STP / positioning (MARK5120) — PV segment, private vs HA | **Yes** — private-pay early adopters (symptomatic, younger, disease-modification-focused) vs broader HA population pre-formulary; positioning differs by channel |
| Comparative / value-based claims vs HU, phlebotomy | **Yes — data-supported only.** HKAPI §3.5: comparative claims require adequate, well-controlled, statistically significant studies naming the comparator. Supported: vs HU — CHR 54.5% vs 34.9% at 6y (p=0.02), EFS 94% vs 82% (p=0.04); vs phlebotomy alone (low-risk PV) — hematocrit control 81% vs 51% (p<0.001), fewer phlebotomies 2.9 vs 4.2/year. **No anagrelide superiority claims without cited RCT.** **No IFN-to-IFN superiority** (Pegasys, China ropeg, traditional IFN) — no head-to-head data |
| EVC / budget impact for HA formulary dossier | **Yes — core formulary work, PV-only for now.** Budget impact on ~500 PV patients; ET excluded from current model (investigational/off-label in HK). Track ET as future market-sizing input — regional data suggests ET may be more prevalent than PV |
| Porter / competitive response (IFN shortage, Pegasys) | **Yes, lightweight** — standing watch on supply disruptions; adjust messaging/availability positioning reactively |
| RICE / impact-confidence-effort | **Yes, informal** — no numeric scoring every quarter; weigh impact-on-formulary vs effort/compliance risk when prioritizing initiatives |
| Patient journey mapping (private → PSP → formulary) | **Yes — central** — private diagnosis → PSP-supported treatment → formulary-listed public access; master narrative for HCP and internal planning |
| Compliance risk triage (UMAO/HKAPI) | **Per-claim screen** — every comparative or value claim checked for: statistical significance, named comparator, no unqualified superlatives ("safe," "best") before external use |
