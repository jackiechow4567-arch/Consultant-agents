Module 1 — Product identity and on-label boundary

Brand / INNBesremi® / ropeginterferon alfa-2b
FormSC injection, prefilled syringe 500 mcg/mL (1 mL); 50–500 mcg adjustable
Excipients (known effect)Benzyl alcohol 10 mg/mL; polysorbate 80 0.05 mg/mL
HK indicationMonotherapy in adults for polycythaemia vera without symptomatic splenomegaly
Potency warning (PI): do not compare potency to another pegylated or non-pegylated protein of the same class. No “stronger than Pegasys,” no China-ropeg rank, no unit-for-unit IFN conversion.
Titration honesty: mean titration ~3.7 months (ropeg) vs ~2.6 months (HU). HU may be preferred when early count reduction is needed. Do not sell “faster than HU.”

ET, MF, and other non-PV indications are not in the HK PI. Reactive scientific exchange only: the HCP asked; you log it in Veeva; you cite current PI; you do not leave ET slides behind.

Local PM authority: pricing execution, PSP, HA dossier packaging, HK compliance. Zero authority to originate scientific claims. If a sentence is not in PI + global lexicon, it does not go on a slide.

Module 4 — Dosing and safety (field-safe, not a protocol)

Start100 mcg SC; 50 mcg if already on other cytoreductive therapy
Titration+50 mcg every 2 weeks; taper other cytoreductive in parallel
TargetsHct <45%, platelets <400×10⁹/L, WBC <10×10⁹/L
Max500 mcg every 2 weeks
Maintenanceq2w ≥1.5 years at stable dose; may then extend to q4w

Contraindication hand-off list: decompensated cirrhosis (Child-Pugh B/C); ESRD GFR <15; severe psychiatric history; severe uncontrolled CV disease; autoimmune disease; transplant immunosuppression; telbivudine combination; hypersensitivity.

ADR: Drug Office adr.html. Never bury safety in a brand conversation.

Module 7 — “Why your drug?” without a Code breach

  1. Licensed HK indication — not the same object as off-label class IFN.
  2. Named comparators only: HU and (where relevant) phlebotomy, using cleared numbers.
  3. Refuse IFN-to-IFN efficacy/potency ranking.
  4. Practical: titration schedule, possible q4w after 1.5y, PSP as currently written.
  5. Access: private + PSP now; HA listing is the goal — no date promise.

Supply disruption of another IFN is an operational fact, not an efficacy claim. China ropeg is a perception watch-item, not a superiority slide.
