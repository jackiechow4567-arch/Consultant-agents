### Q1 — B
HK PI: monotherapy in adults for polycythaemia vera **without symptomatic splenomegaly**.

### Q2 — B
Prefilled syringe 500 mcg/mL (1 mL), SC, 50–500 mcg adjustable.

### Q3 — A
Start **50 mcg** if already on other cytoreductive therapy; otherwise 100 mcg.

### Q4 — B
After ≥1.5 years at a stable q2w dose, interval **may** extend to q4w. Not automatic, not a promise at initiation.

### Q5 — C  *(hard constraint)*
PI potency warning: do not compare potency to other pegylated or non-pegylated proteins of the same therapeutic class.

### Q6 — B
Mean titration ~**3.7 months** ropeg vs ~**2.6 months** HU. Do not sell “faster than HU.”

### Q7 — C
CHR at ~72 months: **54.5% vs 34.9%** (p=0.02). 81 vs 51 is Low-PV Hct control. 94 vs 82 is EFS. 43.1% is PI 12-month disease response (ropeg arm).

### Q19 — model answer (score 1 if all four beats appear)

**Required beats:**

1. Licensed HK indication (adult PV, no symptomatic splenomegaly) — not the same object as off-label class IFN.
2. Named, allowed comparators only: HU (CHR / EFS / molecular) and, if relevant, phlebotomy (Low-PV). Quote only lexicon/PI numbers.
3. **Explicit refusal** of IFN-to-IFN efficacy or potency ranking (Pegasys / China ropeg).
4. Practical: dosing interval, PSP **as written**, HA listing as goal not a promise.

**Fail the item if** the answer claims BESREMi is “stronger,” “best IFN,” or “proven superior to Pegasys.”

**Example:**  
“BESREMi is licensed here as monotherapy for adults with PV without symptomatic splenomegaly — that is a different regulatory object from off-label pegylated IFN. What I can put on the table is RCT evidence versus hydroxyurea, including six-year CHR 54.5% versus 34.9% and event-free survival 94% versus 82%, plus Low-PV hematocrit control versus phlebotomy alone where that population applies. I will not rank us against Pegasys or China ropeg on efficacy; our PI forbids potency comparisons within the class and we have no head-to-head. On practicalities: subcutaneous titration to a two-week schedule, possible four-week maintenance after a stable year and a half, and PSP terms I can confirm with market access rather than invent. Public listing is the access goal; I will not promise a date.”

### Q20 — model answer (score 1 if tie-break + no informal extension)

**Required beats:**

1. **Do not** quietly extend Private Sector 2+1 past expiry for one KOL.
2. Tie-break: **compliance first**, then formulary/public access — a side deal undermines both.
3. Escalate to **Legal/Compliance** and **market access/finance** (and GM if P&L/exception policy is needed). Do not freelance.
4. Redirect energy to HA dossier / transition messaging, not a shadow PSP.

**Example:**  
“Do not keep 2+1 alive off-policy for one KOL. Compliance beats timeline pressure and revenue in our tie-break, and informal PSP changes are an explicit don’t. I will tell market access and Legal that the published Private Sector term ends end-2026, and that any successor program needs Code-aligned patient-assistance review plus written eligibility — not a handshake. In parallel I will keep HA evidence packaging as the #1 milestone so we are not using illegal hospitality-by-discount as a listing substitute. If HQ wants a formal post-2026 affordability option, GM plus finance own the P&L; I will not originate it in the field.”
