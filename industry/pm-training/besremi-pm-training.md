# BESREMi Product Manager training pack

**Audience:** You, as HK Product Manager.  
**Job of this pack:** Make you fluent in what the product *is*, what you may *say*, how patients *access* it, and how you *decide*.  
**Not the job of this pack:** Turn you into a hematologist, invent global claims, or rehearse ET/MF promotion.

**Sensitivity:** Mid. All numbers below are already in this vault (`role-pm.md` + `pm-reference/`). Treat list price, PSP structure, and ~500-patient planning figure as internal planning facts — not slide copy unless globally cleared.

**Recommendation for how to study:** Do the eight modules in order; do not skip Module 1 and Module 6. Then sit the closed-book test. Do not study from competitor-IFN superiority talking points — there is no head-to-head data, and the HK PI forbids potency comparisons across the IFN class.

## Module 1 — Product identity and on-label boundary

### What it is

| Item | HK fact |
|------|---------|
| Brand / INN | Besremi® / ropeginterferon alfa-2b |
| Form | Solution for injection, prefilled syringe **500 mcg/mL** (1 mL), SC |
| Dose delivery | 50–500 mcg adjustable increments on the same syringe |
| Excipients with known effect | Benzyl alcohol 10 mg/mL; polysorbate 80 0.05 mg/mL |

### HK indication (the wall you cannot climb)

**Monotherapy in adults** for **polycythaemia vera without symptomatic splenomegaly**.

Everything proactive — detail aid, advisory board, KOL dinner conversation you initiated, PSP flyer — stays inside that sentence.

### Two PI sentences that save you from compliance incidents

1. **Potency warning:** *"The potency of this medicinal product should not be compared to that of another pegylated or non-pegylated protein of the same therapeutic class."*  
   **PM translation:** No “stronger than Pegasys,” no “better than China ropeg,” no unit-for-unit IFN class conversion story.

2. **Titration honesty:** Mean titration ~**3.7 months** for ropeginterferon vs ~**2.6 months** for hydroxycarbamide. HU may be preferred when **early** count reduction is needed to prevent thrombosis/bleeding.  
   **PM translation:** Do not sell “faster than HU.” Sell durable control and disease-modifying evidence where the lexicon allows. If a physician needs speed, that is a clinical choice — not a positioning failure you paper over.

### Off-label wall

| Topic | Allowed? |
|-------|----------|
| PV (on-label, no symptomatic splenomegaly) | Proactive |
| Essential thrombocythaemia (ET) | **Reactive only** — document in Veeva; cite current local PI; do not leave ET slides behind |
| Myelofibrosis (MF) / other MPN | **Reactive only** — same rules |
| Pregnancy case reports | Not promo material |

**Reactive** means the HCP asked. You did not seed it. You log it. You do not expand it into a mini-symposium.

### Why this module exists

Your local authority is pricing execution, PSP, HA dossier packaging, and HK compliance. You have **zero** authority to originate scientific claims. The PI + global lexicon are the claim boundary. If a sentence is not there, it does not go on a slide.

## Module 2 — PV as a commercial problem (not a textbook)

### Unmet need (UVP block — MGMT6501W)

PV is a myeloproliferative neoplasm driven in most patients by **JAK2V617F**. Standard tools in HK conversations:

- **Phlebotomy** — controls hematocrit, does not address the clone; burden on patients and clinics.
- **Hydroxyurea (HU / hydroxycarbamide)** — first-line cytoreductive for many; faster titration; long-term event and molecular data lag ropeg in PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV.
- **Traditional / other pegylated interferons** — used off-label or as class alternatives; **no head-to-head vs BESREMi**. Price questions will come every call.
- **Ruxolitinib and other cytoreductives** — watch formulary standing; not your IFN-class comparator.

### HK scale (planning assumptions — flag if outdated)

- ~**500** PV patients in Hong Kong.
- Post–October 2025 HK launch; you own local commercialization.
- List price in role memory: **HK$36,000 per injection**.
- **#1 milestone:** HA Drug Advisory Board + Drug List inclusion. Private market is a bridge, not the destination.

### Patient journey (master narrative)

```
Private diagnosis / specialist Rx
        → PSP-supported treatment (affordability on-ramp)
              → HA formulary-listed public access
```

**STP (MARK5120) — two targets, one product, different messages:**

| Segment | Who | What they buy | What you do **not** say |
|---------|-----|---------------|-------------------------|
| Private early adopters | Younger / symptomatic / disease-modification-minded patients and their hematologists; HKSH and private practices | Durable Hct control, fewer phlebotomies, q2w→q4w convenience, JAK2 story where lexicon-cleared | “Best IFN”; ET expansion; HA-reimbursed as if already listed |
| Broader HA population (pre-listing) | Cluster hospitals; budget holders | Cost-effectiveness, budget impact on ~500 patients, licensed indication fit, safety | Volume-only arguments in a year HA drug spend fell |

**Positioning statement (working, lexicon-bound):**  
*For adults with PV without symptomatic splenomegaly who need cytoreduction beyond phlebotomy, BESREMi is a licensed ropeginterferon alfa-2b monotherapy that has RCT evidence versus HU (long-term CHR / EFS / molecular response) and versus phlebotomy alone in low-risk PV — not an IFN-class potency play.*

### Channel map (no PII)

| Channel | Sites (public names only) | Access reality |
|---------|---------------------------|----------------|
| Private | Hong Kong Sanatorium & Hospital; private hematology | PSP Private Sector (buy 2 get 1, first year, expires end 2026) |
| Public / HA | QMH, PYEH, PWH, QEH, UCH, TKOH, TMH | Formulary path is the north star; do not promise listing |

## Module 3 — Evidence you may use (and the wall around it)

**Rule:** Comparative claims need an adequate, well-controlled, statistically significant study that **names the comparator** (HKAPI §3.5). Then they still need **global lexicon + local PI** clearance before a slide exists.

### Claim card (memorize this table)

| Comparator | Head-to-head in HK promo? | Core numbers (published / PI) | Use for |
|------------|---------------------------|-------------------------------|---------|
| Hydroxyurea | **Yes, if citing named RCT** | CONTINUATION-PV ~6y: CHR **54.5% vs 34.9%** (p=0.02); EFS **0.94 vs 0.82** (p=0.04); JAK2 molecular response **66.0% vs 19.4%** (p<0.0001) | Disease-modifying / vs-HU narrative |
| Phlebotomy alone (low-risk PV) | **Yes, if citing named RCT** | Low-PV: Hct control **81% vs 51%**; phlebotomy burden **2.9 vs 4.2 / year** (role-memory lexicon figures) | Early-intervention / phlebotomy-burden narrative |
| Pegasys / other IFN / China ropeg | **No superiority** | None. PI potency warning. | Availability, licensed PV indication, dosing convenience — never efficacy rank |
| Anagrelide | **No** | No cited RCT in this library | Do not invent |

### Study identities (so you can name them)

**PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV** (Gisslinger et al., *Leukemia* 2023)

- Phase III, ropeginterferon alfa-2b vs HU (control → BAT allowed in extension); n=254 at randomization (1:1) in the PI summary.
- PI 12-month disease response (ropeg arm): **43.1%** — a PI efficacy figure; the **6-year comparative** numbers are the usual vs-HU story.
- Risk events at 6y (library summary): 5/95 (5.3%) ropeg vs 12/74 (16.2%) control (thrombosis, MF, AML, death mix — quote only as the paper/PI allow).

**Low-PV** (Barbui et al., *NEJM Evidence* 2023)

- Phase 2, open-label, ropeg 100 mcg q2w vs phlebotomy-only; n=127.
- Primary: Hct ≤45% over 12 months — **81% vs 51%**.

**PARADIGM (Phase 4):** lifecycle data source in role memory. Do **not** expand locally beyond cleared lexicon materials.

**Taiwan / regional ET epidemiology:** future market-sizing only. Not in the current EVC model. Raw advisory-board text is High sensitivity — stays in `work-briefs/`.

### Hard-won commercial lesson

Physicians will ask why BESREMi costs many times more than traditional IFN used off-label. Without head-to-head data you **cannot** claim superior efficacy vs Pegasys or China ropeg. Differentiation stays inside approved PV evidence, dosing convenience, licensed indication, and lexicon-approved disease-modifying narrative.

## Module 4 — Dosing and safety (enough to not freeze on a call)

You do not titrate patients. You must not get the basics wrong when a hematologist stress-tests you, and you must hand clinical judgment to Medical.

### Dosing (HK PI summary)

| Phase | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Start | **100 mcg** SC; **50 mcg** if already on other cytoreductive therapy |
| Titration | **+50 mcg every 2 weeks**; taper the other cytoreductive in parallel |
| Targets | Hct **<45%**, platelets **<400×10⁹/L**, WBC **<10×10⁹/L** |
| Maximum | **500 mcg every 2 weeks** |
| Maintenance | Stay q2w for **≥1.5 years** at a stable dose; may then extend to **q4w** |

**Commercial implication of q4w:** convenience and injection-cost trajectory for PSP/EVC — not a promise you make before the PI conditions are met.

### Key contraindications (hand-off list, not a counseling script)

Decompensated cirrhosis (Child-Pugh B/C); ESRD (GFR <15); severe psychiatric history; severe uncontrolled CV disease; autoimmune disease; transplant immunosuppression; combination with telbivudine; hypersensitivity.

### ADR

HCPs report via Drug Office ADR Online Reporting System: http://www.drugoffice.gov.hk/adr.html  
Your job: never bury a safety question in a brand conversation. Route to PV / Medical. Log reactive exchanges.

### HADF mechanics (enough to brief HQ)

From Operation Guideline v21.4 (w.e.f. 25 Apr 2026):

| Type | Practical meaning |
|------|-------------------|
| General | Use as clinically indicated |
| Special | Listed indication; first Rx usually needs specialist authorization (default 12-month validity) |
| SFI / safety net | Self-financed / CCF categories — budget-impact model must match the listing type you are seeking |

You do **not** invent which listing type HA will grant. You package PV-only EVC for ~500 patients. ET stays out of the current model.

### HA budget climate (public)

### Longer-range access watch

CDCC / Community Drug Formulary / community pharmacy (2025 HKAPI primary-care session) is a **separate** track from acute HADF. Do not conflate them in external messaging without HQ alignment.

### Tie-break when access tactics conflict (from role memory)

**Compliance → Formulary / public access → HQ strategic fit → Affordability → Timeline → Revenue → Team capacity.**

PSP expiry end-2026 is a forcing function for private-to-public transition messaging — not a reason to loosen Code rules.

## Module 6 — Compliance operating system (UMAO + HKAPI)

This module outranks revenue. If a tactic fights the Code, the tactic dies.

### Instruments

| Instrument | Why you care |
|------------|--------------|
| **UMAO (Cap. 231)** | All HCP/patient-facing advertising |
| **HKAPI Code of Practice (21st ed.)** | Promo, hospitality, PSP, symposia, HCP contracts |
| **PPB ADR guidance** | Safety reporting timelines and forms |
| **Cap. 486 PDPO** | Patient/HCP data in PSP and ADR files |
| **Global lexicon** | Substance of every PV claim |

### Promo materials (HKAPI §4)

Every promotional piece carries **abbreviated PI**: approved indication, dosage, contraindications, side effects, **and the date of approval**.

### Comparative claims (HKAPI §3.5)

Named comparator + adequate well-controlled statistically significant study. No unqualified superlatives (“safe,” “best”).

### Hospitality

- Breakfast/lunch: **HK$400 / attendee**
- Dinner: **HK$800 / attendee**
- No entertainment (theatre, concerts, sports). Meals incidental only.

### Advisory boards / symposia (HKAPI §5)

- ≥ **two-thirds** genuine scientific content.
- Agenda documented and distributed **in advance**.
- HCP advisors: **written contract**, legitimate need, **fair-market-value** fee set in advance. No undocumented “thank you.”

### Promotional items (HKAPI §6.2)

**No product-branded items** to HCPs for a prescription-only medicine (calendars, mouse pads, sticky notes, pens-with-logo, etc.).

### AI-drafted copy (2026 CoP TTT)

AI can draft. A human still runs lexicon + UMAO/HKAPI review. The model is not the signatory.

### PSP term changes

Align with HKAPI patient-assistance norms. Private Sector expiry is not a loophole.

### Promo review checklist (use on every asset)

1. On-label PV only in proactive materials  
2. Claim traceable to PI + global lexicon + named study if comparative  
3. Abbreviated PI block present (with approval date)  
4. No branded giveaways; hospitality within caps  
5. No ET/MF proactive content  
6. Sensitivity tier tagged

## Module 7 — Competition and the standing “why your drug?” answer

### Watchlist (do not turn into attack ads)

- Pegasys / IFN **supply disruptions** — availability is a legitimate operational fact; it is not an efficacy claim.
- **China ropeginterferon** — regional approval/pricing can leak into HK physician and cross-border patient perception.
- **HU generics, ruxolitinib, other cytoreductives** — formulary standing of alternatives changes the HA story.

### Compliant response framework (memorize the shape, not a rogue slogan)

When asked “Why pay this vs off-label IFN?”:

1. **Indication:** BESREMi is licensed in HK as monotherapy for adult PV without symptomatic splenomegaly. Class IFN use is not the same regulatory object.
2. **Evidence vs named, studied comparators:** HU (PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV) and phlebotomy (Low-PV) — quote only cleared numbers.
3. **What you will not do:** Rank BESREMi above Pegasys or China ropeg on efficacy. PI potency warning.
4. **Practical:** Dosing (q2w, possible q4w after ≥1.5y stable), PFS presentation, PSP eligibility **as currently written**.
5. **Access path:** Private + PSP now; HA listing is the public-access goal — no promise of date or outcome.

### Competitive intent vs fairness

Price objections are not solved by a superlative. They are solved by a legal claim set + an affordability program that you do not freelance.

Recommendation first. Options. Risks. Next steps. One page.
