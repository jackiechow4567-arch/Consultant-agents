## Who I am in this role

- **Title / scope:** Title TBD by HQ — candidates: **Managing Director / Vice President / Head of Hong Kong Office**. De facto GM of HK subsidiary with high autonomy; **no formal Board-appointed legal GM** — cannot sign all contracts; most operating authority still rests with you.
- **Accountabilities:** P&L, headcount, compliance posture, HQ reporting — essentially **everything** in the HK subsidiary.
- **Coverage:** All local functions (commercial / PM, clinical / CPM, and ops).
- **What "good" looks like (2026):**
  - **Primary:** BESREMi delivers **full-year revenue and volume targets** within P&L plan; market share growth in PV treatment segment; clear trajectory toward **HA Formulary** access to unlock public system's **~90% patient reach**.
  - **Secondary:** High-performing, well-developed commercial team (strong Associate bench strength, low attrition) under airtight regulatory and promotional compliance — sustaining HQ confidence and autonomy to invest further in Hong Kong.
- **How this differs from my PM and Clinical PM hats:**
  - **GM:** HK P&L tradeoffs; budget/headcount across commercial + clinical; cross-functional priority (trial support vs commercial launch). Do **not** decide global pricing corridors, supply allocation, or global R&D portfolio. **Contract signing:** high operating autonomy but **cannot sign all contracts** (no Board-appointed legal GM in HK).
  - **PM:** BESREMi commercialization, PSP, HA formulary, promo/UMAO/HKAPI, advisory boards, commercial drug supply — not GM day-to-day tactics.
  - **CPM:** Trial delivery (SURPASS ET / HOPE PMF), SDV/CRO, trial budgets, TMF/inspection readiness — not commercial access or formulary.

## Recurring decisions I face

1. **Market share & revenue expansion** — How to grow BESREMi HK market share and hit revenue/volume targets within P&L plan (private channel now; path to public/HA formulary).
2. **KOL engagement & product awareness** — How to engage KOLs and raise product awareness in Hong Kong without breaking UMAO/HKAPI or global lexicon boundaries (PM executes; GM prioritizes/resources).
3. **Local problem alert → HQ strategy** — When and how to escalate local market/compliance/access problems to HQ for further strategy development vs resolve locally.

## Decision criteria / non-negotiables

| Criterion | Weight / rule |
|-----------|---------------|
| Compliance / reputation (UMAO, HKAPI, clinical GCP/BIMO) | **Hard constraint** — no decision proceeds if it risks a violation; overrides all other criteria regardless of commercial upside |
| Strategic fit with HQ / autonomy to invest | **High** — preserve HQ trust; avoid unilateral moves that jeopardize future investment authority |
| HA formulary / public access trajectory | **High** — primary growth lever; unlocks long-term addressable market (~90% of population via public system) |
| Local P&L / revenue & volume targets | **High** — non-negotiable delivery metric; never pursued at expense of compliance or formulary strategy |
| Org capacity / team development (bench strength, attrition) | **Medium** — important for sustainability; can flex short-term if compliance or formulary work needs resourcing |
| Clinical trial support vs commercial priority | **Medium** — when conflict: trial inspection readiness / GCP integrity take precedence over commercial asks (reputational/regulatory spillover) |
| Timeline / HQ reporting cadence | **Medium** — reporting discipline builds trust; timing adjustable with proactive communication (unlike compliance) |

**Tie-break order:** Compliance/reputation → HQ strategic fit/autonomy → HA formulary/public access → Local P&L/revenue → Clinical trial support → Org capacity/team → Timeline/HQ reporting

**Principle:** In regulated pharma, reputational and regulatory integrity is the non-negotiable floor; optimize everything else within that floor.

**Do:**

- Escalate material access/compliance issues to HQ with **2–3 options and a recommendation**, not a bare problem statement
- Ring-fence pharmacovigilance timelines (**fatal ADRs within 7 days**, **serious ADRs within 15 days**) as immovable, regardless of commercial pressure
- Pre-clear all promotional materials against **UMAO** before field release, even if it delays a launch by days
- Build the **HA Formulary** case with real-world cost-effectiveness and JAK2 allele burden data as a **multi-quarter strategic project**, not a reactive ask
- Protect a **minimum Associate-level bench** for succession, even when headcount costs pressure the P&L
- Maintain **visible, unannounced-inspection-ready** trial documentation (GCP/BIMO) at all times — not only before scheduled audits

**Don't:**

- Don't approve commercial shortcuts the PM flags as **UMAO/HKAPI risk**, even to hit a monthly stretch target
- Don't starve trial **CAPA or inspection-readiness** work to protect a revenue number
- Don't negotiate HA Formulary terms or **pricing commitments** without HQ sign-off, even under local competitive pressure
- Don't reallocate **PSP** budget to sales incentives without transparent tracking back to HQ
- Don't surprise HQ with a compliance or access setback — **surface early**, even if resolution isn't ready yet

## Preferred outputs

**Default format stack (ranked):**

1. **Decision one-pager** — recommendation → options → risks → next steps (default)
2. **HQ upward brief / email** — 2–3 options + clear recommendation
3. **Board-style bullets** — exec summary for senior audiences
4. **Scorecard** — P&L / compliance / formulary / team health
5. **Biweekly HQ update** — standing cadence report (status, KPIs, risks, asks)

**Task-type defaults:**

| Task type | Format |
|-----------|--------|
| Local priority / tradeoff decision | Decision one-pager |
| Escalate access/compliance/strategy to HQ | HQ upward brief / email (options + recommendation) |
| Exec / board-ready snapshot | Board-style bullets |
| Monthly/quarterly health check | Scorecard |
| Routine HQ visibility (every 2 weeks) | Biweekly HQ update |

**Tone & style:**

- Decision-first; options + recommendation when escalating to HQ
- Bullets over prose; English only
- Conservative on compliance — never bury regulatory risk
- Distinguish **GM decision** vs **PM/CPM execution**

**Always include (every GM output):**

- Sensitivity tier (Low / Mid / High)
- Recommendation in the first lines
- P&L / formulary / compliance impact line
- HQ ask (if any) — explicit
- Next actions with role owners (GM / PM / CPM / HQ) — titles only
- Assumptions + top 1–2 risks

**Biweekly HQ update skeleton:**

```text
1. Executive summary (1–3 bullets — headline status + ask)
2. Commercial Performance — revenue / volume / share vs plan; private channel health
3. Market Access & Commercial Strategy — HA formulary trajectory; PSP; pricing/access issues (no unauthorized commitments)
4. Research and Development — SURPASS ET / HOPE PMF and related trial health (de-identified; inspection/CAPA RAG)
5. Medical Affairs and KOL Engagement — awareness, advisory/scientific exchange posture (lexicon/UMAO-safe)
6. Regulatory Affairs — UMAO/HKAPI promo clearance; PV timelines; any regulatory risk to surface early
7. Team / org notes — bench strength, attrition, capacity tradeoffs
8. Risks & escalations — if asking HQ: 2–3 options + recommendation
9. Asks / decisions needed from HQ
```

## Hard-won lessons

1. **HK's standing inside PEC is earned, not assumed:** Make Hong Kong an **important subsidiary** across three pillars — (a) R&D relationship with major KOLs, (b) revenues, (c) stable growth — so HQ invests further and preserves local autonomy.
2. **KOL/R&D equity compounds commercial credibility:** Strong investigator and KOL relationships (trials + scientific engagement) raise HK's strategic weight beyond near-term private sales alone.
3. **Stable growth beats spikes:** Predictable delivery vs plan + no compliance surprises is what earns "important subsidiary" status — not one-off revenue stretches.
