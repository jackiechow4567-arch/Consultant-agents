Piece A · Pathophysiology

JAK2 signaling without EPO

Normal

EPO binds the EPO receptor → receptor homodimerization → JAK2 autophosphorylation → ordered red-cell production.

Mutated JAK2 (PV)

Constitutive JAK2 signaling in the absence of EPO. That is the biologic rationale for disease-directed cytoreduction, not hematocrit snapshots alone.

Bridge to treatment goals: prevent thrombotic events, prevent myelofibrosis, prevent acute leukemia — and improve symptoms (Piece A).

Piece A · WHO 2016

Diagnostic criteria for PV

Major

  1. Hemoglobin >16.5 g/dL (M) / >16.0 (F) or hematocrit >49% (M) / >48% (F) or red-cell mass >25% above predicted
  2. Bone-marrow biopsy: erythroid + granulocytic + megakaryocytic proliferation
  3. Gene: JAK2 V617F or JAK2 exon 12

Minor

Subnormal serum erythropoietin.

Requirement

All 3 major or first 2 major + the minor criterion.

Piece B · PEC-BES-HK020126

NCCN low-risk PV — US context

Say out loud: this is NCCN, not the Hong Kong PI and not HA Drug Formulary.

Initial management

  • Manage cardiovascular risk
  • Aspirin 81–100 mg/day
  • Phlebotomy — hematocrit <45%

If cytoreduction indicated

Preferred (NCCN): clinical trial or ropeginterferon alfa-2b-njft
Other: hydroxyurea or peginterferon alfa-2a

Indications to start cytoreduction

  • New thrombosis or bleeding
  • Frequent or poorly tolerated phlebotomy
  • Progressive thrombocytosis or leukocytosis
  • Splenomegaly
  • Disease symptoms — pruritus, night sweats, fatigue

HK: if symptomatic splenomegaly, do not proactively position BESREMi.

Piece B · PEC-BES-HK020126

NCCN high-risk PV — US context

Start with cytoreduction

Same aspirin + phlebotomy backbone, plus:

  • Preferred: hydroxyurea or ropeginterferon alfa-2b-njft
  • Other: peginterferon alfa-2a
  • Certain circumstances: ruxolitinib

If inadequate / lost response

  1. Clinical trial or ruxolitinib (NCCN category 1 after HU resistance/intolerance in high-risk PV)
  2. Other if not previously used: ropeg, HU, or peginterferon alfa-2a

Do not convert this into a BESREMi-vs-ruxolitinib superiority claim.

Hong Kong PI

What to prescribe — BESREMi

Indication

Monotherapy in adults for polycythaemia vera without symptomatic splenomegaly.

Form

SC prefilled syringe 500 mcg/mL (1 mL); 50–500 mcg adjustable.

Dosing

Start100 mcg q2w (50 mcg if already on cytoreductive)
Titrate+50 mcg every 2 weeks; taper the other agent
TargetsHct <45%; Plt <400; WBC <10 × 10⁹/L
Max / maintain500 mcg q2w; after ≥1.5 y stable may go q4w

Contraindications include decompensated cirrhosis, ESRD GFR <15, severe psychiatric disease, severe uncontrolled CV disease, autoimmune disease, transplant immunosuppression, telbivudine, hypersensitivity. ADR: drugoffice.gov.hk/adr.html
