# Pegasys difference — claim check (HK PM)

> **Sensitivity:** Mid (published labels + papers). Not an approved leave-behind.  
> **Question checked (2026-08-20):** “Pegasys dose is 3× lower (180 vs 500 mcg); we had Hep B/C H2H years ago with fewer flu-like symptoms; PROUD/CONTI patients used product up to 6 years.”  
> **Verdict for clinic:** Keep **labelled schedules** and **6-year PV follow-up**. Do **not** convert mcg ratios or hepatitis H2H into a PV superiority story.

## Why the “3× dose” line fails (even before compliance)

1. **Interval.** Pegasys 180 mcg is **weekly**. BESREMi 500 mcg is **every 2 weeks**.
2. **Cap vs typical.** Many PV patients on BESREMi stabilize **below** 500 mcg (mean ~350–380 mcg in the Phase 3 program). Many MPN clinicians use **off-label Pegasys at 45–90 mcg weekly**, not 180.
3. **Potency ≠ micrograms.** At the **same 180 mcg weekly**, ropeginterferon showed **greater exposure** than peginterferon alfa-2a in the HCV Phase 2 PK subset. That is exactly why labels say not to compare potency.
4. **HK/EMA PI (Section 2 / 5.1):** *“The potency of this medicinal product should not be compared to that of another pegylated or non-pegylated protein of the same therapeutic class.”*

HKAPI §3.5 still requires a named, adequate, statistically significant study **in the claim you are making**. A hepatitis trial does not license a PV tolerability comparison.

## Clinic script (if they ask “how is this different from Pegasys?”)

**Say:**

“They are different molecules and different labels. BESREMi is indicated in Hong Kong for **adult PV without symptomatic splenomegaly**, dosed **every two weeks**, titrated to blood-count targets, **maximum 500 micrograms**. Pegasys is peginterferon alfa-2a; its classic labelled adult hepatitis dose is **180 micrograms once weekly**. Microgram numbers are **not** interchangeable — our PI says potency should not be compared across this class. We do **not** have a PV head-to-head versus Pegasys. What we do have in PV is PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV versus hydroxyurea, with patients followed **through six years**. Flu-like symptoms remain an interferon-class effect on our PV label; I will not claim we have fewer flu-like events than Pegasys in PV.”

**Never say:**

- “Pegasys is three times weaker / three times lower dose.”
- “500 is our optimal dose; they only use 180.”
- “We already beat Pegasys in Hep B and C, so we have fewer flu-like symptoms in PV.”
- “Phase 3 hepatitis was versus Pegasys” (it was **PEG-Intron**).

**If they press for the old hepatitis papers:** park to **Medical Information**; log Veeva; do not volunteer the papers.
