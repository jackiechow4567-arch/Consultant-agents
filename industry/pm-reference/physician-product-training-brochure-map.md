# Why the training numbers differ from the physician brochure

> **Answer first:** They are the **same trials** (PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV). The brochure and the v2 training quote **different analyses** of those trials. Training was built from Piece A, Piece B (`PEC-BES-HK020126`), the HK PI, and Gisslinger *Leukemia* 2023. It was **not** built from this full EFFICACY / SAFETY / OTHER INFO leave-behind. If you walk this brochure while quoting the training’s “lead numbers,” you will look inconsistent.  
> **Sensitivity:** Mid — approved-style physician brochure + published RCT + HK PI.  
> **Status:** Internal map for 26 Aug. Still DRAFT for field until Medical ticks the brochure as the live leave-behind.

**Do** treat this brochure as the HCP leave-behind and use training as a **translation layer** (name the analysis on each page). **Do not** replace the brochure’s graph with 54.5%, and **do not** let 84.3% or the year-1 graph (~62%) be spoken as if they were the HK PI 12-month primary (43.1% vs 46%).

## The CHR collision (this is the one that will get you in the room)

The brochure page *“BESREMi achieved significant and sustained hematologic control”* and the training are both “CHR vs HU/BAT.” They are **not the same percentage**.

| Number the physician will see or hear | Source | What the analysis actually is | What it is not |
|---------------------------------------|--------|-------------------------------|----------------|
| **~62% vs ~75% at month 12** on the green/yellow graph | Brochure 5-year CHR curve | Snapshot / LOCF-style curve: HU is **faster at 1 year**, then the lines cross | **Not** the HK PI 12-month primary |
| **43.1% vs 46.0%, p=0.63** | HK PI Clinical Studies; PROUD-PV ITT | Primary 12-month disease response; **discontinued = non-responder**; non-inferiority **not met** | Not the graph’s month-12 dots |
| **~74% vs ~57% at 24 mo; ~77% vs ~54% at 36 mo; ~73% vs ~53% at 60 mo** | Same brochure graph | Same curve after the crossover; “sustained through 5 years” on the artwork | Not Gisslinger 2023 **primary** 72-month CHR |
| **84.3% (107/127)** | Brochure callout, 36 months | **Overall / ever-response:** criteria met **at any time** up to cutoff (footnote on the page) | **Not** “84% still in CHR at 3 years” |
| **54.5% vs 34.9%, p=0.02** | Training lead; Gisslinger 2023; PI 72-month lineage | Primary late CHR; discontinued = non-responder (48/88 vs 22/63) | Not the graph and not 84.3% |
| **72.6% vs 47.3%** | Gisslinger 2023 LOCF sensitivity | Closest **published** analogue to the ~70–80% graph | Not a third “real” response rate to mix in the same sentence |
| **3% (4/127) major TE at 36 months** | Brochure callout | 36-month incidence on the **n=127** randomized set | **Not** the EFS 5/95 first-event count (different cohort, longer follow-up, composite) |

**Talk-track when the brochure is open:**

1. Point at the graph: “This curve is long-term CHR **among the analysis used on this page**. Hydroxyurea is ahead at 12 months. Ropeg crosses by 24 months and holds through 60 months.”
2. Then, without attacking the page: “The **registered 12-month primary** in the HK PI is **43.1% vs 46%** — that counts discontinuations as failures. That is why a haematologist who only remembers PROUD-PV year 1 will say it ‘lost.’ Both statements can be true.”
3. If they point at **84.3%**: “That is **ever** achieved CHR by 36 months (107/127), not the snapshot on the graph and not the 72-month primary.”
4. If they ask for the PI-aligned late number: **54.5% vs 34.9% at 72 months, p=0.02.**

**Do not say:** “About 80% of patients respond” as a single headline. The brochure is allowed to show 84.3% **with its footnote**; you must say the footnote out loud.

## Why training was written that way (intent, not an error in the papers)

1. **HK PI is the binding local claim source.** The 12-month **43.1%** and the 72-month **54.5%** sit in the PI / Gisslinger primary. HKAPI comparative claims need named, significant results — 54.5% vs 34.9% (p=0.02) and EFS p=0.04 qualify; year-1 does not.
2. **Piece B** already had a ~70–80% CHR visual. Training labelled it LOCF so field would not say “80% respond.” This **new** brochure goes further: it prints **84.3%** and a month-12 graph **above** the PI primary. Without a translation script, field will either (a) contradict the leave-behind or (b) over-claim.
3. **Molecular and the 36-month AE table were not on Piece B**, so v2 parked JAK2 as optional and summarised safety as TRAE/D/C. This brochure makes both **core** whenever the piece is handed over.

That is a **PM/compliance sequencing** choice, not evidence that Gisslinger and the brochure disagree on whether ropeg beats HU long-term. They agree on direction. They disagree on **which percentage you are allowed to treat as the headline**.
