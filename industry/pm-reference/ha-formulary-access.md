## HADF mechanics (Operation Guideline v21.4, w.e.f. 25 Apr 2026)

| Drug type | Authorization | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **General** | Use as clinically indicated | No authorization required |
| **Special** | Listed indication(s); first Rx needs **authorization** (usually specialist grade); default **12-month** validity | Can prescribe outside listed indication as HADF "other indication" or as **self-financed item (SFI)** per rules |
| **SFI / safety net** | wSN(SF), wSN(CCF) categories | Budget impact modeling must align to correct listing type |

**Key notes for dossier work:**

- Indications for Special/SFI drugs = **licensed indication(s)** and/or current HA practice
- Unregistered drugs: corporate HADF listing vs hospital temporary named-patient procedures differ
- Therapeutic classification follows **BNF 68** (PV/MPN → typically **BNF 9 Nutrition and blood** / malignant disease areas — confirm in full HADF listing)
- Expert Panels + Drug Formulary Committee review operation guideline periodically

## HA financial context (HKAPI Executive Digest 1/2026 — public sources)
