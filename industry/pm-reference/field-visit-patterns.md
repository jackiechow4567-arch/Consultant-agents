# Field-visit patterns (de-identified) — BESREMi HK PM

> **Sensitivity:** Mid (aggregated). Raw CRM with HCP names, phones, emails, and patient-level notes is **High** — do not copy into this file or into git.  
> **Source period:** Mar–Aug 2026 private + HA contacts (one PM).  
> **Use:** Recalibrate physician training. Not an approved leave-behind.

## What the field actually tested (not the academic deck)

Across ~20 contacts, physicians and nurses almost never opened with PROUD-PV p-values. They opened with **price vs off-label peg-IFN / China IFN**, **syringe wastage at 100 mcg**, **who injects**, and **whether supply of the cheap alternative is stable**.

| Pattern | How often it showed up | Training implication |
|---------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Affordability = **Barrier** (not “manageable”) | Nearly every HCP row | PSP math must be said in **one sentence**; do not imply HQ will cut list price |
| Current SoC = **Pegasys experience** or **China IFN** | Private haematology majority | Frame of reference is **off-label IFN replacement**, not HU-first |
| Pegasys **back in HK** (distributor restocked); off-label for PV | Repeated environmental note | Availability is **no longer a shortage-only story**; residual POD is **supply reliability**, not “they have no IFN” |
| China / Xiamen IFN as working SoC | At least one busy private centre | Third comparator: even cheaper than Pegasys; still **no IFN-to-IFN superiority** |
| Skeptical → Neutral → first use | One private haematologist over 4 months | Conversion is **multi-visit + nurse + first-patient follow-up**, not one close |
| Nurse as gatekeeper | Majority of private first calls | Separate **nurse visit** (device, brochure, callback rule) vs **physician visit** (label, access) |
| Interest in **250 mcg / accelerated titration** | Neutral / used as “cheaper SKU” | **Medical/RA owned.** Labelled start remains 100 mcg (50 if on other cytoreductive). Do not sell 250 as a discount protocol unless the SKU is registered **and** the protocol is lexicon-cleared |
| Pen / titration wastage (“100 mcg from a 500 pack”) | Early private KOL | Know **local IFU**: adjustable PFS vs single-use discard; **do not promise a pen** unless registered in HK |
| q2w → q4w “to save cost” | Used once; physician said **Pegasys can extend interval too** | Retire cost-save q4w vs Pegasys. q4w is a **labelled option after ≥1.5 y stable**, not a price weapon |
| ET / pre-MF / MF “more feasible” / ET filing date | HA consultant + private centre | **Hard stop.** HK PI is PV only. Pipeline dates are not local claims |
| Congress / ASH–EHA sponsorship | Multiple KOLs | Compliance process (need, FMV, contract) — **not** a matching war with other companies’ trip budgets |
| First patient: mild sleep complaint, causality unclear | One starter | **PV first**, then product talk |
| HA junior: “when will you lower the price?” | One resident | No price-cut promise; reflect to HQ **internally**, not as a field commitment |
| HA senior: wants to try + HADF/PSP-in-HA logic + IIT | One consultant | Split **commercial PV** vs **Clinical PM / CTC** for IIT |

## Competitive economics (order of magnitude only)

Do not quote a named clinic’s acquisition cost as a company claim. Use **ranges** in clinic:

- BESREMi planning list remains on the order of **HK$36,000 / injection**; Private Sector PSP (buy 2 get 1, private, first year, **ends 2026**) still leaves a **large** residual vs off-label peg-IFN.
- Private haematologists describe **Pegasys syringes in the low-thousands HKD** and **China IFN lower still** (sometimes framed as “~10×”).
- Clinic **overhead on the selling price** can make the patient-facing number higher than list-minus-PSP. Confirm current PSP quote with market access **before** the visit — do not invent a net.

**Compliant POD vs cheap IFN:** Hong Kong **PV-specific indication**, q2w labelled schedule, **named long-term vs-HU data**, **local supply you can actually describe**, PSP **rules**. Not potency, not flu-like vs Pegasys, not “we will match their price.”

## Lines to retire (observed in field)

| Said or implied | Why it fails |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Another doctor’s titration (e.g. 250 / 350 / 500) as **the** way to use BESREMi | Anecdote ≠ PI; sounds like off-protocol coaching |
| q4w after 1.5 years **to save cost vs Pegasys** | They can extend peg-IFN interval too; PI is clinical stability, not a discount |
| “I am persuading HQ to lower the price” | Creates a wait-for-cut objection; HQ pricing is not a local promise |
| ET approval “expected after US FDA, around [month]” | Unapproved indication + timeline = proactive off-label |
| MF / pre-fibrotic MF “more feasible to prescribe” | Direct off-label invitation |
| 250 mcg as **the cheaper dose option** without RA/pricing clearance | SKU + price claim |
| Promise of a **pen** during titration | Device must be on the HK pack list |

## Two visit types (design the training this way)

**Type A — Private IFN clinic (physician + nurse)**  
Objective: one **on-label PV** patient who can pay (or PSP-eligible), nurse competent to inject, first-patient follow-up booked.  
Do not: ET volume, congress as the closer, HQ price cut.

**Type B — HA consultant**  
Objective: evidence questions for **HADF**, who is an appropriate **self-pay / named** patient today, IIT parked to **Clinical PM**.  
Do not: “use it for MF because they have nothing.”
