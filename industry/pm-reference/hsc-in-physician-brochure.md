> **Role lens:** Product Manager (BESREMi / PV)  
> **Source pieces:** Piece A (MPN/PV disease board) and Piece B (`PEC-BES-HK020126`, *Rediscovering Polycythemia Vera*). Artwork not copied into git.

## Recommendation (answer first)

**Do** treat **HSCs = hematopoietic stem cells** — the bone-marrow cells of origin for PV — and use that as **disease-biology context** for why JAK2 and molecular response appear on the board. **Do not** convert the HSC diagram into a promotional claim that BESREMi uniquely “kills HSCs,” “cures the clone,” or “prevents myelofibrosis / leukemia.”

## What HSCs are (plain language)

**Hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs)** are rare cells in the bone marrow that generate all blood lineages: red cells, white cells, and platelets.

In polycythemia vera, an **acquired JAK2 mutation** (most often V617F; less often exon 12) arises in an HSC. That mutant HSC and its progeny keep signaling **without needing EPO**, so the marrow overproduces erythrocytes (and often leukocytes and platelets). That is the “neoplastic JAK2-driven proliferation” already on Piece A.

| Term on the board | Means | Not the same as |
|-------------------|--------|-----------------|
| **HSC / HSCs** | Hematopoietic stem cell(s) — cell of origin | HSCT (hematopoietic stem-cell **transplant**) |
| **JAK2 V617F / exon 12** | Driver mutation inside those cells | A BESREMi-specific biomarker claim |
| **EPO-independent signaling** | Mutant JAK2 fires without erythropoietin | “BESREMi blocks JAK2” (wrong mechanism class) |

Public medical language from PharmaEssentia’s PV materials matches this: PV is caused by mutation in hematopoietic stem cells in the bone marrow, leading to overproduction of blood cells. The US **patient** brochure says this in lay terms (“a single stem cell… JAK2”). That US patient wording is **not** automatically a Hong Kong HCP claim.

## Where this sits on the physician pieces

**Piece A (disease board)** — HSC language belongs in the **JAK2 pathophysiology** panel next to:

- Normal: EPO → EPO receptor → JAK2 autophosphorylation  
- PV: JAK2 mutation → constitutive signaling **without** EPO  
- WHO 2016 marrow criterion: trilineage (erythroid / granulocytic / megakaryocytic) proliferation  

If the artwork shows a cascade **HSC → progenitors → mature blood cells**, that is classification biology, not a second indication. Keep **~80% of time on the PV column**. ET/PMF columns remain differential only.

**Piece B (`PEC-BES-HK020126`)** — CHR / EFS / NCCN pathways. HSCs are usually **not** the efficacy headline here. Do not drag HSC biology across to justify NCCN “preferred,” the HU 10-year risk table, or the ~70–80% CHR graph.

## What you may say vs what you may not

**Say (biology, then stop):**  
“PV starts in a mutated hematopoietic stem cell — an HSC — with JAK2 driving blood-cell production even without EPO. That is why we talk about counts **and** the clone, not phlebotomy alone as a long-term plan in patients who meet cytoreduction criteria.”

**Say (efficacy, named data only):**  
CONTINUATION-PV / PI: CHR **54.5% vs 34.9%** at 72 months (p=0.02); EFS **0.94 vs 0.82** (p=0.04). Molecular response **66.0% vs 19.4%** at 6 years (p<0.0001) only if the live global lexicon allows that line in this module.

**Never say from the HSC diagram:**

- “BESREMi targets / depletes / eradicates HSCs” as a product claim  
- “This is stem-cell therapy” or confusion with **HSCT**  
- “Prevents MF / AML / death” (EFS is a composite; do not unbundle it)  
- IFN-to-IFN superiority because interferon is “more HSC-directed” than Pegasys / China ropeg / traditional IFN  
- US patient-site slogans (“targets the cause,” “only FDA-approved treatment that targets the bone marrow”) as HK promotional copy  
- ET or MF HSC biology as a reason to start BESREMi (not in HK PI — reactive Medical only)

**Mechanism reminder:** Interferon alfa signals through **IFNAR / JAK1 / TYK2 / STAT**, not by being a JAK2 inhibitor. Do not describe BESREMi as if it were ruxolitinib.

## Field 20-second version

“HSCs are hematopoietic stem cells — the marrow cells that make blood. In PV a JAK2 mutation in an HSC makes too many red cells without EPO. The board is disease biology. For BESREMi I stay on the Hong Kong indication and the named hydroxyurea / phlebotomy studies — I will not claim we uniquely wipe out the stem-cell clone.”

## Risks

1. **Ethical fading:** “Just explaining the picture” slides into “we work at the stem-cell level, HU does not.” Mitigant: STOP–BRIDGE–CITE; efficacy only from named RCTs.  
2. **HSCT confusion:** A transplant-oriented colleague hears “HSC” and thinks transplant. Mitigant: say **hematopoietic stem cells**, not transplant.
