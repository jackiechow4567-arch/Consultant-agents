# MPD-RC 112 (Pegasys vs HU) vs PROUD / CONTINUATION-PV (ropeg vs HU)

> **Use:** Reactive scientific exchange when an HCP says “I’ll just use Pegasys.”  
> **Not for:** Promotional IFN-to-IFN superiority, detail aids, or HA dossier comparative claims vs peginterferon alfa-2a.  
> **Sources:** Mascarenhas et al., *Blood* 2022;139(19):2931–2941 (DOI 10.1182/blood.2021012743; NCT01259856); Gisslinger et al. PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV as already distilled in `pv-clinical-evidence.md` and HK PI.  
> **Sensitivity:** Mid (published trials + commercial objection handling). Keep PSP uptake and unpublished KOL names out of HCP materials.

## Claim boundaries (read first)

| Allowed | Not allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Name each **trial** and its **own** comparator (HU) | “Besremi is more effective / safer / better tolerated than Pegasys” |
| Design, endpoint, follow-up, and power differences | Cross-trial CHR / JAK2 / AE rate as if head-to-head |
| HK PI: Besremi is **PV monotherapy** (no symptomatic splenomegaly) | Using MPD-RC 112 ET results in **proactive** PV promotion |
| HKAPI §3.5: comparative claims only vs **named RCT comparator** | Potency / mcg-to-mcg equivalence (HK PI §2 / §5.1 warning) |
| Dosing interval and on-label vs off-label as **product facts** | Inventing HA listing status for Pegasys in PV |

**Hard rule (role-pm):** no IFN-to-IFN superiority. Frame of reference stays **cytoreduction for PV** (HU / IFN class / phlebotomy), not a Pegasys battle card.

## One-line PM read

MPD-RC 112 does **not** make Pegasys equivalent to Besremi. It shows weekly peg-IFN-α-2a and HU are both active at 12 months in a mixed **ET+PV**, **underpowered**, **supply-stopped** trial, with **no event difference** and **more grade 3/4 AEs** on PEG. The Besremi edge vs that objection is **label + PV-only 6-year vs-HU evidence + dosing/supply**, not a cross-trial efficacy win.

## Side-by-side (do not promote as head-to-head)

| Dimension | MPD-RC 112 (Mascarenhas, *Blood* 2022) | PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV (lexicon / PI) |
|-----------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| Drug | peg-rIFN-α-2a (Pegasys), Roche-supplied | ropeginterferon alfa-2b (Besremi) |
| Population | Treatment-naïve **high-risk ET or PV** (WHO 2008) | **PV only** |
| n randomized | 168 (87 PV / 81 ET); planned 300 | Phase III ~254–257; extension continued |
| Dosing | 45 µg weekly → max **180 µg weekly** (median ~89 µg/week) | 100 µg q2w (50 µg if already on cytoreduction) → max **500 µg q2w**; may extend **q4w** after ≥1.5 y stable |
| Primary endpoint | ELN **CR at 12 months**: counts **+ spleen + symptom resolution** | 12-month CHR **+ normal spleen** (non-inferiority vs HU) |
| 12-month primary | **Failed superiority:** CR 35% PEG vs 37% HU, **p=0.80** | **Did not meet non-inferiority** on composite with spleen (paper cites 21.3% vs 27.6% in 245/257). PI 12-month disease response **43.1%** (ropeg arm) |
| Later haematologic | 24 mo ORR 60% vs 41% (p=0.045); 36 mo CR 33% vs 17% (**n=57**, NS) | **72 mo CHR 54.5% vs 34.9%** (p=0.02) |
| Events / EFS | **5 events** total; HR 0.60 (0.10–3.62); 24-mo thrombosis **2% vs 2%** | **72 mo EFS 0.94 vs 0.82** (p=0.04); HR 0.34 (0.12–0.97) |
| JAK2V617F | PEG reduced VAF through 24 mo; HU rose after 12 mo (interaction **p=0.002**); median max Δ **−10.7% vs −5.3%** | 6-year ELN molecular response **66.0% vs 19.4%** (p<0.0001) |
| PV slice (112 only) | 12-mo CR **28% PEG vs 30% HU**; Hct control without phlebotomy **65% vs 43% (p=0.04)** | PV-only programme by design |
| Safety (112) | Grade ≥3 AE **46% PEG vs 28% HU (p=0.01)**; more flu-like, injection-site, depression, leukopenia | Use **Besremi PI** only — do not imply safer than Pegasys |
| Integrity / follow-up | Closed after 168 pts because **manufacturer halted PEG access**; 56% discontinued for study closure; conditional power **12%** had it gone to 300 | Commercial product; ~6-year randomized extension vs HU / BAT |
| HK label | Pegasys **not** a PV indication in Besremi PI / local promo boundary | **On-label** adult PV without symptomatic splenomegaly |

### CR definition trap (use this; do not claim higher CR)

MPD-RC 112 CR required **symptom resolution** and **ultrasound spleen** (splenomegaly >13 cm). PROUD-style CHR did **not**. The *Blood* authors themselves say this “may account for their higher CR rates.”  
**Field use:** if an HCP equates 12-month CR across trials, correct the endpoint mix — do not counter with a bigger Besremi percentage as “proof vs Pegasys.”

### *Blood* discussion wording vs PROUD primary

Mascarenhas et al. wrote that Ro-PEG “was not inferior” to HU at 12 months. The PROUD-PV **primary non-inferiority endpoint (CHR + spleen) was not met**. Benefit vs HU in the lexicon is the **long-term CONTINUATION-PV / 6-year** package, not a 12-month NI win. Correct gently if a KOL quotes the *Blood* sentence.

### FAQ — “At month 12, is Pegasys vs HU worse than ropeg vs HU?”

**No. Do not say that.** Month 12 is the **wrong** place to claim a Besremi edge over Pegasys.

| 12-month readout | Pegasys vs HU (MPD-RC 112) | Ropeg vs HU (PROUD-PV) |
|------------------|----------------------------|-------------------------|
| Primary / composite | CR **35% vs 37%**, p=0.80 — **no difference** | CHR + spleen **21.3% vs 27.6%** — **NI not met** (HU numerically higher) |
| PV counts | PV CR **28% vs 30%**; Hct control **65% vs 43%, p=0.04** (**PEG better**) | PI 12-month disease response **43.1%** (ropeg arm); HU often preferred for **early** control (titration ~2.6 vs 3.7 mo) |

Read each IFN **only against HU in its own trial**. Cross-trial 35% vs 21% is invalid (different CR definitions, ET mixed in, different NI vs superiority designs).

**What month 12 actually says (class, not brand):** neither IFN beat HU on a strict 12-month composite. If an HCP argues Pegasys is “already as good as HU at 1 year,” 112 supports that for CR; it does **not** mean Pegasys lost to ropeg.

**Where Besremi *does* separate from the Pegasys *evidence package*:** not month 12, but **completed PV-only follow-up vs HU to 6 years (CHR + EFS)** plus label / q2w dosing / supply. 112 never delivered that long-term vs-HU event analysis.

## What is a real edge when physicians mention Pegasys

Use in this order (strongest → weakest for **compliant** conversation):

1. **Regulatory / practice:** Besremi is the IFN **licensed for PV** in HK. Pegasys PV use is **off-label**. Do not invent HA formulary status for Pegasys; point to licensed indication and named-patient / SFI reality as a question for the institution.
2. **Evidence that matches the PV decision:** PV-only Phase III vs HU with **6-year CHR + EFS + JAK2** in the global lexicon. MPD-RC 112 is mixed ET/PV, 12-month primary negative, too few events to test thrombosis/progression, and stopped for supply.
3. **Time course (class, not brand war):** Both programmes show IFN is **not a 12-month HU-killer**. MPD-RC 112: no CR difference at 12 months; signal at 24 months ORR. PROUD: 12-month composite NI missed; 6-year CHR/EFS vs HU. Aligns with HK PI: mean titration **~3.7 mo** ropeg vs **~2.6 mo** HU — HU may be preferred when **early** count reduction is needed.
4. **Dosing / administration (product fact):** weekly Pegasys vs q2w Besremi, with possible q4w after ≥1.5 years. Convenience and persistence — **not** efficacy vs Pegasys.
5. **Supply as published history, not a smear:** MPD-RC 112 closed because PEG access was halted. Standing watch item in `role-pm.md`. Phrase as “trial integrity / access,” not “Roche abandoned patients.”
6. **Low-risk PV:** Low-PV vs phlebotomy is a **Besremi** lexicon story. MPD-RC 112 did **not** study low-risk PV. Do not imply Pegasys failed there — it was not tested.

## What is **not** an edge (HCPs will push back)

- **12-month PV haematocrit control in 112 favoured PEG (65% vs 43%, p=0.04).** Acknowledge. Reframe to duration and event data in **each** programme; do not deny the finding.
- **Molecular response in 112 also favoured PEG vs HU at 24 months.** Same class story as ropeg vs HU. Using it to say “Pegasys already does JAK2” is fair from the HCP; your counter is **PV-only duration, EFS, label, dose interval** — not “our JAK2 % is bigger.”
- **Price vs off-label IFN.** No H2H outcomes. Value = licensed PV evidence + dosing + PSP (Base Case buy-5-get-1; Private Sector buy-2-get-1, private only, first year, **expires end 2026**) — never quote unsupported comparative efficacy to justify list price.
- **Safety vs Pegasys.** 112 shows more grade ≥3 AEs on weekly PEG **vs HU**. That is not a Besremi vs Pegasys safety claim.

## Field script (reactive, 60–90 seconds)

> “MPD-RC 112 is an important investigator trial of **weekly Pegasys versus HU** in **high-risk ET and PV**. At 12 months, complete response was **not** different (35% vs 37%). The study mixed two diseases, used a stricter CR definition including symptoms and spleen, was **stopped early when Pegasys supply was withdrawn**, and had only **five** thrombotic or progression events — so it cannot tell us which drug prevents clots or MF. Grade 3/4 AEs were higher on PEG than HU.  
> Besremi is a **different molecule**, **licensed for PV** here, studied in a **PV-only** Phase III against HU out to **six years**, with lexicon CHR and event-free survival versus HU. We do **not** have a head-to-head versus Pegasys, and the PI says not to compare potency across pegylated interferons. If the question is first-line cytoreduction in PV, the evidence package I can discuss on-label is Besremi versus HU and, in low-risk disease, versus phlebotomy — not versus Pegasys.”

If they press ET: **reactive only** — MPD-RC 112 included ET; Besremi HK PI does not. Log in Veeva.

## HA formulary impact

- **Helps the dossier:** PV-specific long-term vs-HU package; licensed indication (HADF Special/SFI listed indications = licensed ± HA practice).
- **Does not help:** a “Pegasys alternative” cost slide. HA will treat off-label IFN as a **price** challenge, not as a reason to drop Besremi evidence quality.
- Route any new comparative wording through **global Medical/Regulatory** before local adaptation.
