# Ropeg vs peginterferon — claim-safe PM comparison

> Distilled for HK BESREMi Product Manager use. Public molecule / label / published-trial facts = **Low**. Local commercial tactics (PSP terms, list-price assumption) stay in `role-pm.md` (**Mid** — do not paste to Perplexity).  
> **Hard boundary:** no IFN-to-IFN superiority claims (efficacy, safety, or potency). HK PI potency warning + no head-to-head RCT vs Pegasys / other pegIFN / China-origin ropeg.

## What HCPs usually mean

| Term in the room | Typical product | IFN backbone | Pegylation | Usual MPN use |
|------------------|-----------------|--------------|------------|----------------|
| **Ropeg / BESREMi / P1101** | ropeginterferon alfa-2b | IFN **alfa-2b** + proline linker | **Site-specific, mono-PEGylated, single isoform** | **On-label PV** (HK/US/EU and other approved markets) |
| **Peginterferon / PEG-IFN / Pegasys** | peginterferon alfa-2a | IFN **alfa-2a** | Random / multi-isoform PEGylation (branched PEG) | **Off-label** PV/ET/MF in many Asian centres; labelled for viral hepatitis, not PV |
| **PegIntron-class pegIFN** | peginterferon alfa-2b | IFN **alfa-2b** | Smaller linear PEG; multiple isoforms | Historical MPN / hepatitis use; not the HK PV on-label product |
| **Conventional / “traditional” IFN** | non-pegylated IFN alfa-2a/2b | Unpegylated | None | Frequent injections; poorer convenience/tolerability vs pegylated products |

AMWG (2023) treats **peg-IFNα-2a or ropeg-IFNα-2b** as the preferred interferon options in PV when cytoreduction is indicated (younger patients, pregnancy-planning, HU resistance/intolerance, selected low-risk PV). That is **class guidance**, not a head-to-head ranking.

## Product differences that are fair to state (not comparative efficacy)

| Dimension | Ropeginterferon alfa-2b (BESREMi) | Peginterferon alfa-2a (typical “pegIFN”) |
|-----------|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Molecule** | Site-specific **mono-PEGylated proline-IFN alfa-2b**; chemically **homogeneous single isoform** (Verstovsek 2022 protocol paper; Edahiro 2022) | Random PEGylation → **mixture of positional isoforms**, each with its own activity/stability (same papers contrast this with earlier-generation pegIFN) |
| **Dosing interval** | Start/titrate **every 2 weeks**; after ≥1.5 y at stable dose, HK PI allows extension to **every 4 weeks** | Typically **weekly** SC in MPN practice (HK RWE: Gill 2020 started PEG-IFNα-2A at **135 µg SC**) |
| **HK on-label** | **Yes** — monotherapy in adults with PV **without symptomatic splenomegaly** | **No** — PV use is off-label scientific exchange / clinical practice, not a licensed PV indication |
| **Pivotal evidence frame** | Phase 3 **vs hydroxyurea** (PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV); Low-PV **vs phlebotomy** | MPN evidence is largely single-arm, vs HU (not vs ropeg), or real-world (incl. HK PEG-IFNα-2A series). **No named H2H RCT vs ropeg** in the PM library |
| **Potency / dose conversion** | HK PI: *“The potency of this medicinal product should not be compared to that of another pegylated or non-pegylated protein of the same therapeutic class.”* | Same class warning applies in reverse — **do not mcg-to-mcg convert** or imply equivalent/superior effect |
| **Disease-modification narrative** | Allowed **only** via named, lexicon-cleared studies vs **HU** or **phlebotomy** (CHR, EFS, JAK2 burden — see `pv-clinical-evidence.md`) | IFN class can reduce JAK2 burden in published MPN literature; **cannot** be used to claim BESREMi is a better molecular therapy than pegIFN |
| **Access / formulary** | Licensed PV indication is the HADF-relevant listing basis | Off-label pegIFN is a **practice/supply** alternative, not a like-for-like listed PV brand |

## Evidence you may cite vs evidence you may not

**May cite (named comparator, published, lexicon-aligned):**

- vs **HU**: CONTINUATION-PV ~6 y — CHR **54.5% vs 34.9%** (p=0.02); EFS **0.94 vs 0.82** (p=0.04) — Gisslinger et al., *Leukemia* 2023
- vs **phlebotomy** (low-risk PV): Hct control **81% vs 51%** — Barbui et al., *NEJM Evidence* 2023
- HK PI dosing, indication, contraindications, titration vs HU (mean titration ~**3.7 vs 2.6 months** — clinical nuance, not an attack line)

**Must not cite as promotional / proactive HCP claims:**

- Any **superiority vs Pegasys, pegIFN-2b, conventional IFN, or China-origin ropeg** (no H2H; HKAPI §3.5)
- Edahiro 2022 language that a single isoform is “*expected* to have a better safety profile” — mechanistic **hypothesis**, not a cleared comparative claim
- SURPASS-ET / ET / MF / pregnancy case series as **proactive** PV promotion — **reactive-only** per current HK PI
- Gill 2020 PEG-IFNα-2A HK RWE as a BESREMi comparator (different products, mixed MPN, not H2H)

## Compliant answer to “why not cheaper pegIFN?”

Use this frame; do not improvise comparative outcomes:

1. **Same therapeutic class, not the same product.** Potency is not interchangeable (HK PI).
2. **Label:** BESREMi is the interferon **licensed in HK for adult PV** (without symptomatic splenomegaly). PegIFN-α2a PV use is off-label.
3. **Evidence package:** Phase 3 and long-term data are **versus HU** (and Low-PV vs phlebotomy), not versus other interferons.
4. **Use:** q2w (possible q4w maintenance) prefilled-syringe titration is a **convenience / administration** difference, not a proof of better efficacy.
5. **Access:** PSP (see `role-pm.md`) bridges private affordability; public sustainability is **HA formulary**, which keys off **licensed indication + cost-effectiveness**, not off-label pegIFN price.

## PM implications (local)

- **Positioning FOR:** on-label PV interferon; disease modification vs HU/phlebotomy; dosing interval; single-isoform product identity; supply/label reliability.
- **Positioning AGAINST (do not use):** “better interferon than Pegasys.”
- **Competitive watch:** Pegasys / IFN **supply disruption** (availability message, not efficacy); **China-origin ropeg** perception among cross-border patients.
- **Formulary:** licensed PV indication is an access *advantage vs off-label pegIFN*; still must win on EVC/budget impact vs HU and other listed cytoreductives — not on IFN-class price.
