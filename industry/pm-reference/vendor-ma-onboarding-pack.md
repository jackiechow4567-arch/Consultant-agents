## Recommendation

**Send a Wave 1 public reading pack now (HK PI + the 8 must-read publications below). Do not email named-patient cases, PSP/pricing, unpublished HA materials, or unapproved training slides until the CDA/agreement is executed and Medical has cleared Wave 2.**

Why:

1. The vendor asked to self-study *while the agreement is being finalised* — public papers and the approved PI meet that need without leaking patient or commercial data.
2. Their five topic bullets are a Medical-led training agenda, not a document dump. Disease, MoA, evidence, and positioning can be pre-read; HK named-patient experience cannot travel by email.
3. HKAPI/UMAO and Cap. 486 still apply once a vendor is in the loop. Sending case-level files or unapproved claims now is the highest-probability failure mode.

## Claim-safe answers to the vendor’s five bullets

Use in Medical training. Do not paste the **High** rows into the vendor email.

### 1. Polycythaemia vera and the current treatment pathway

- **Disease:** BCR-ABL-negative MPN; almost all patients have **JAK2V617F** (or rare JAK2 exon 12). Morbidity is thrombosis, bleeding, symptom burden, and risk of MF/AML.
- **Risk:** High-risk = age &gt;60 **or** prior thrombosis. Low-risk = neither.
- **Foundation for all patients:** low-dose aspirin (if no contraindication) + **Hct &lt;45%** (CYTO-PV).
- **Low-risk first line:** phlebotomy ± aspirin. ELN 2021: start cytoreduction if phlebotomy intolerance, inadequate Hct control, progressive splenomegaly, persistent/progressive leukocytosis, extreme thrombocytosis, high CV risk, or high symptom burden. **Preferred cytoreductive in younger patients needing treatment: recombinant IFN-α (ropeginterferon alfa-2b or peg-IFN-α-2a).**
- **High-risk first line:** cytoreduction — **HU or IFN**; NCCN also lists ropeginterferon as a preferred option in both risk groups (confirm current NCCN version in training; do not redistribute NCCN unless licensed).
- **Second line after HU resistance/intolerance:** IFN or **ruxolitinib** (RESPONSE; ruxolitinib is the JAK1/2 inhibitor slot, especially with splenomegaly/symptoms).
- **HK access reality (Wave 2, not Wave 1 email):** private market first; public listing is the HA Drug Formulary milestone. ~500 PV patients is an internal planning assumption — do not quote as epidemiology in vendor materials unless sourced.

### 2. BESREMi mechanism, indication, dosing (HK PI)

| Item | HK PI language (summarise; quote PI in training) |
|------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Product | Besremi® **ropeginterferon alfa-2b**, 500 mcg/mL prefilled syringe, SC |
| Indication | **Monotherapy in adults** for **polycythaemia vera without symptomatic splenomegaly** |
| Start | **100 mcg** q2w; **50 mcg** if already on another cytoreductive; +**50 mcg** every 2 weeks while tapering the other agent |
| Targets | Hct &lt;45%, platelets &lt;400×10⁹/L, WBC &lt;10×10⁹/L |
| Max | **500 mcg** q2w |
| Maintenance | q2w for ≥1.5 y at stable dose; may extend to **q4w** |
| MoA (scientific) | Type I IFN; longer half-life from monopegylation → q2w (then q4w) dosing; activity against the **JAK2V617F** clone (Qin 2024). |
| Potency warning | *“The potency of this medicinal product should not be compared to that of another pegylated or non-pegylated protein of the same therapeutic class.”* → **no IFN-to-IFN superiority claims** (Pegasys, China ropeg, traditional IFN). |
| Titration nuance | Mean titration ~**3.7 months** vs ~**2.6 months** for HU; HU may be preferred when **early** count reduction is needed to prevent thrombosis/bleeding. |
| Key contraindications | Decompensated cirrhosis (Child-Pugh B/C); ESRD (GFR &lt;15); severe psychiatric history; severe uncontrolled CV disease; autoimmune disease; transplant immunosuppression; telbivudine; hypersensitivity. Benzyl alcohol / polysorbate 80 in formulation. |

**HIDAT (250–350–500 µg)** is **not** the Hong Kong labelled titration. Medical may mention it as overseas/scientific data; all HK access and HCP work must use **local PI dosing**.

### 4. Product positioning versus current options

**Frame of reference (MARK5120):** adult PV needing cytoreduction (or low-risk patients failing Hct control with phlebotomy), **without symptomatic splenomegaly** (HK indication).

| Option | Role in pathway | BESREMi POD (points of difference) — data-supported only | Do not say |
|--------|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------|------------|
| Phlebotomy ± aspirin | Foundation; often sufficient in uncomplicated low-risk | Low-PV: better sustained Hct control; lower phlebotomy burden in responders | “All low-risk patients should start BESREMi” |
| Hydroxyurea | Most used public-sector cytoreductive; faster early count drop | Named RCT: higher long-term CHR, molecular response, EFS; non-leukaemogenic IFN class narrative **only if lexicon-cleared**; q2w/q4w SC vs daily oral | Unqualified “safer” / “best”; ignore the slower titration vs HU |
| Peg-IFN-α-2a (Pegasys) / traditional IFN / China ropeg | Same therapeutic class; off-label or different product | Dosing convenience (q2w → q4w); **approved PV indication** in HK; PI potency warning | Any IFN-to-IFN **superiority** (efficacy, safety, or potency) |
| Ruxolitinib | HU-resistant/intolerant; splenomegaly / symptom control (RESPONSE) | BESREMi is **not** positioned as a spleen-shrink JAK inhibitor; HK indication **excludes symptomatic splenomegaly** | Head-to-head or “replace Jakavi” |
| Anagrelide | Platelet-focused; not PV disease-modifying standard | Out of BESREMi’s comparative claim set | Superiority without RCT |

**HK commercial positioning (internal, Wave 2):** private-pay, disease-modification-focused patients first (PSP-supported) → HA formulary as the public-access north star. Vendor work must stay inside this on-label, lexicon-aligned frame.

### 5. Previous named-patient cases and physician experience in Hong Kong

**Do not email cases.** This bullet is **High** (patient-adjacent, re-identification risk, Cap. 486).
