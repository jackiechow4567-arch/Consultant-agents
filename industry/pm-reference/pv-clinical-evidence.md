## PROUD-PV / CONTINUATION-PV (Gisslinger et al., Leukemia 2023)

- **Design:** Phase III, ropeginterferon alfa-2b vs HU (control → BAT allowed in extension); ~6 years total
- **Event-free survival (72 mo):** **0.94 vs 0.82** (log-rank **p=0.04**); HR **0.34** (95% CI 0.12–0.97)
- **CHR at 72 mo:** **54.5% vs 34.9%** (RR 1.55, 95% CI 1.07–2.26, **p=0.02**)
- **Risk events:** 5/95 (5.3%) ropeg vs 12/74 (16.2%) control (thrombosis, MF, AML, death mix)
- **Molecular (JAK2V617F):** 66.0% molecular response (ELN) vs 19.4% control at 6y (p<0.0001)
- **PM use:** Core disease-modifying / vs-HU narrative for formulary and lexicon-aligned materials

## Low-risk PV vs phlebotomy (Barbui et al., NEJM Evidence 2023)

- **Design:** Phase 2, open-label, ropeg 100 mcg q2w vs phlebotomy-only; n=127
- **Primary (Hct ≤45% over 12 mo):** **81% vs 51%**
- **Symptoms / splenomegaly:** Lower moderate/severe symptoms and splenomegaly in ropeg responders
- **Phlebotomy burden:** Non-responders switched to ropeg needed high phlebotomy (4.7/patient/year cited in abstract context)
- **PM use:** Early-intervention / phlebotomy-burden narrative where lexicon permits
