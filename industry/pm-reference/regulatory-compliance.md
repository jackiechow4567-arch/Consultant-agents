## HKAPI rules already in `role-pm.md` (operational)

### Comparative claims (Section 3.5)

- Permitted when based on **adequate, well-controlled, statistically significant** studies **naming the comparator**
- Apply to **HU** and **phlebotomy** only where RCT cited — not IFN class comparisons

### Hospitality (meal caps)

- Breakfast/lunch: **HK$400/attendee**
- Dinner: **HK$800/attendee**

### Promotional materials (Section 4)

- Include **abbreviated PI**: indication, dosage, contraindications, side effects, **approval date**

### Symposia / advisory boards (Section 5)

- ≥**two-thirds** genuine scientific content; agenda documented and distributed in advance
- **No entertainment** (theatre, concerts, sports) — modest meal-incidental only (§5.3(h))

### Promotional items (Section 6.2)

- **No product-branded items** to HCPs for POM (calendars, mouse pads, sticky notes, etc.)

### HCP consultants (Section 5 / contracts)

- Written contract, legitimate need, **FMV** compensation in advance — no undocumented "thank you" fees

## ADR reporting (PPB / Drug Office)

- Industry must report ADRs per PPB guidance (serious/unexpected timelines in full PDF)
- **Format:** DH ADR form or CIOMS
- **Channel:** Drug Office ADR online — http://www.drugoffice.gov.hk/adr.html
- Reactive HCP exchanges → **Veeva** + pharmacovigilance per `role-pm.md`

## Agent checklist (promo review)

1. On-label PV only in proactive materials  
2. Claim traceable to PI + global lexicon + named study if comparative  
3. Abbreviated PI block present  
4. No branded giveaways; hospitality within caps  
5. No ET/MF proactive content  
6. Sensitivity tier tagged
