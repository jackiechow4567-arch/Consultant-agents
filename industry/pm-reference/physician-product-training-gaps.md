## Gaps ranked (what a HK hematologist will actually ask)

### 1. Year-1 PROUD-PV — the “it failed” objection (critical)

**In the library:** Kiladjian 2022 / Okikiolu citing PROUD-PV month-12 hematologic response **without spleen: 43% vs 46%, p=0.63**. HK PI also lists 12-month disease response **43.1%**.

**In current training:** Only the **72-month win** (CHR 54.5% vs 34.9%, p=0.02). Titration honesty (3.7 vs 2.6 months) is there, but not the year-1 non-significance.

**Gap:** Any hematologist who saw the original PROUD-PV readout will say “it lost to HU at one year.” Unprepared field looks cherry-picking.

**Add (promo-safe if named and balanced):** One slide/speaker note: slower hematologic onset; 12-month composite was **not superior**; long-term CHR and EFS are the reason to wait. Do not hide 43% vs 46%.

---

### 2. Low-PV vs phlebotomy — largest missing RCT (critical, gated)

**Paper:** Barbui et al., *NEJM Evidence* 2023. Phase 2, n=127, ELN/NCCN low-risk, **fixed 100 µg q2w** vs phlebotomy + aspirin.

| Endpoint | Ropeg | Phlebotomy-only |
|----------|-------|-----------------|
| 12-mo composite (Hct ≤45% + no progression) | 52/64 **(81.3%)** | 32/63 **(50.8%)** p&lt;0.001 |
| Phlebotomies / patient-year | **2.9** | **4.2** |
| Progression in 12 mo | **0/64** | **8/63 (12.7%)** including 2 vascular events |

**5-year follow-on (*Ann Hematol*):** KM **drug survival** 72% at 2 y / ~58% at 5 y. The **94%** Hct control at 3–5 years is **among n=36 still on drug**, not ITT. Late switch after phlebotomy failure: only **~30%** then respond at 100 µg. Practical heuristic: **≥3 phlebotomies in the first 6 months** flags phlebotomy-only failure.

**Gap:** Current training has **no answer** to “my patient is &lt;60, no clot — why not venesection only?” NCCN “preferred ropeg in low-risk” is US art, not an RCT vs phlebotomy.

**Add:** Gated module (Medical yes/no). If yes, teach **two different regimens**: Low-PV **fixed 100 µg** vs HK PI **titration**. Never flatten them. Never claim thrombosis prevention from 0 vs 2 events. Never quote 94% as 5-year ITT. Title trap: “drug survival” ≠ overall survival.

### 3. Safety is almost absent (high)

Current training lists contraindications and ADR URL. It does **not** give trial AE rates.

**From Kiladjian 2022 (5-year RCT, n=127/127):** TRAE **78.7% both arms**; grade ≥3 TRAE **16.5% both**; SAE ~24–25%; **drug-related discontinuation 10.2% vs 3.1%** (Gisslinger 2023 letter: **11.0% vs 2.4%** to 7.3 y). Skin cancers related: **0 vs 3** on control.

**From Edahiro 2022 (Japan Ph2, n=29, start 100 µg — PI-like, no symptomatic splenomegaly):** related TEAE 100%; **0 grade ≥3 related**; **alopecia 55.2%** (vs 4.7% cited from PROUD-PV); flu-like 27.6%; fatigue 27.6%; ALT 20.7%. Snapshot CHR 51.7% at 52 w; **durable primary 27.6%**.

**Gap:** Field cannot counsel “what will my patient feel?” or “is this more toxic than HU?” Balanced message: grade ≥3 similar to HU; more IFN-class discontinuations; Asian alopecia counseling if Medical clears Edahiro.

**Do not** add Gill 2020 Pegasys AE tables as if they were BESREMi.

### 4. EFS completeness (medium–high)

Training already has 0.94 vs 0.82, p=0.04, 5/95 vs 12/74. **Missing from the Gisslinger 2023 letter:**

- **EFS ≠ OS.** Median EFS not reached. OS was **not** an endpoint.
- First-event mix: ropeg TE 2 / MF 1 / death 2; control TE 5 / MF 2 / **AML 2** / death 3. Do not turn 0 vs 2 AML into “prevents leukemia.”
- Time with normal WBC **93.7% vs 80.9%** (p=0.02).
- JAK2 VAF &gt;50% in year 6: **11.6% vs 50.0%**.
- Year-6 practice: **61.9%** on q3–4w; median ~499 µg/4 weeks — answers “can I space injections?” inside PI maintenance language.
- Kiladjian **month-60 CHR 55.8% vs 44.0%, p=0.0974 (NS)** — do not let anyone quote “significant CHR at 5 years.”

---

### 5. Asia / HK overlay vs NCCN-only (high for local credibility)

**AMWG 2023** (Gill / Kwong, HKU corresponding): first Asia-adapted classical-MPN consensus. Still not HA listing. Useful overlay:

- IFN class for younger / childbearing-age (age &lt;70 in their wording) — **peg-IFN-2a or ropeg**, no ranking.
- ELN HU-resistance **2 g/day × 3 months “may not apply”** in Asia (intolerance at lower doses).
- MERGE: in Asia **ET &gt; PV &gt; MF** (opposite Western PV-first mix).
- Count targets: AMWG WBC **≤15 × 10⁹/L** vs HK PI **&lt;10** — do not mix.

**Chim 1997 (QMH, n=41, PVSG, pre-JAK2):** only **Hong Kong PV** series in the upload. Median age 62; splenomegaly **68%**; ever-thrombosis **34%**; authors called Chinese PV “relatively benign.” Use as **historical phenotype**, not modern risk or HU-safety vs Piece B’s 10-year figures. Paper internally disagrees on 10-year TFS (**83% vs 72%** in different sections) — do not pick one.

**Edahiro 2022:** best **PI-aligned East-Asian** 1-year dataset. Single-arm; not a vs-HU claim.

**Jin 2023:** China phase II is a **protocol** (n=49 enrolled, **HIDAT 250–350–500**, HU-R/I). **No CHR/AE results in this file.** Do not tell physicians “Chinese patients showed X.”
