# Current ET treatment options in Hong Kong (public sources)

> **Sensitivity:** Low (public HA / Drug Office / published local practice).  
> **Use:** ET indication-application landscape for market access. **Not** HK promotional copy.  
> **As of:** 2026-08-28. HA public search lists **drug names and category**, not the full Special-drug indication footnotes — those must be pulled from the live HADF search or cluster DTC before a dossier is written.  
> **Do not invent** HA committee outcomes or unlisted indications.

## How ET is treated in Hong Kong (practice)

There is **no separate HA “ET protocol” PDF**. Public hospitals follow risk-adapted international practice (ELN / NCCN / Asian Myeloid Working Group), using drugs that are already on the HA Drug Formulary.

**Local published practice (Queen Mary Hospital / HKU):** Gill et al., *Hematology* 2020 — ET patients were treated with conventional therapy (**hydroxyurea ± anagrelide**), **pegylated interferon alfa-2a**, or **ruxolitinib**. PEG-IFN-α-2a had higher clinicohematologic complete response in ET in that cohort. [doi:10.1080/16078454.2020.1780755](https://doi.org/10.1080/16078454.2020.1780755)

**Asian Myeloid Working Group consensus** (Gill corresponding author, QMH): cytoreductives in ET = HU, IFN-α, anagrelide; younger patients and HU-resistant/intolerant patients should be considered for **peg-IFN-α-2a or ropeginterferon alfa-2b**. [PMID 37747591](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37747591/) — this is **clinical consensus**, not a Hong Kong label for ropeginterferon in ET.

## Public-sector toolkit (HA Drug Formulary)

Source: [HA Drug Formulary search](https://www.ha.org.hk/hadf/en/Others/Search-Result.html) (Nutrition & Blood; Malignant disease & Immunosuppression). Categories: General / Special / SFI with safety net / SFI without safety net.

| Option | HADF category (public name list) | Typical ET role in HK | Registration / label notes | Access implication |
|--------|----------------------------------|------------------------|----------------------------|--------------------|
| **Low-dose aspirin** | **General** | All eligible patients (antiplatelet), unless bleeding / acquired VWD | Widely registered antiplatelet | Standard fees |
| **Hydroxyurea (hydroxycarbamide)** | **General** (cytotoxic) | **First-line cytoreduction** for high-risk ET | Long-established cytotoxic; used across MPN | Workhorse of public ET care; no specialist “Special” gate |
| **Anagrelide** | **Special** (Nutrition & Blood — anaemias and other blood disorders) | ET-specific platelet-lowering; usually **second line after HU** (ELN) or selected first line | **Anagrelide Sandoz 0.5 mg** HK-68231 (registered 13 May 2024, POM). Agrylin / other brands: confirm on [Drug Office search](https://www.drugoffice.gov.hk/eps/do/en/consumer/search_drug_database2.html). Global labels: thrombocythaemia secondary to MPN | HA-funded when prescribed under **specified Special conditions** (exact footnote not on the public name list — pull from HADF). Otherwise patient pays |
| **Peginterferon alfa** | **Special** (drugs affecting the immune response) | Used in HK MPN practice (Gill 2020), especially younger / pregnancy-planning / HU-fail | **Pegasys** listed on Drug Office as HK-51529 / HK-51530 (tertiary sources). MIMS HK / local PI: **chronic hepatitis B and C**, not ET | High chance ET use is **outside the HA-listed indication** → “other indication” or SFI (patient pays) unless the cluster footnote includes MPN. **Confirm the Special indication text** |
| **Busulphan (busulfan)** | **Special** (cytotoxic) | Older patients, later-line cytoreduction | Alkylator; leukemogenicity concern | Specialist authorization |
| **Ruxolitinib (Jakavi)** | **SFI with safety net** | **Not an ET disease-modifying standard.** Used for **MF** (including post-ET MF) symptoms/spleen; some MPN real-world use | MIMS Hong Kong: (1) MF-related splenomegaly/symptoms (PMF, post-PV MF, **post-ET MF**); (2) **PV resistant/intolerant to HU**. **No ET indication** | CCF first-phase lists ruxolitinib for **myelofibrosis-related splenomegaly or symptoms** (and GVHD) — [CCF items PDF](https://www.ha.org.hk/haho/ho/ccf/CCF_items_en.pdf). ET itself is not a CCF item |
| **Ropeginterferon alfa-2b (BESREMi)** | **Not listed for ET** | Investigational / off-label for ET in HK | HK PI: **PV without symptomatic splenomegaly** only | Named-patient / trial / private off-label only until an indication variation is approved. Do not present as a current HA ET option |

**HADF reminder:** Special drugs at standard fees only under listed clinical conditions + specialist authorisation. Patients who do not meet the footnote **self-finance**. Unregistered or unlisted uses go through hospital named-patient / temporary procedures, not corporate listing.

## Private sector

Same clinical ladder (aspirin → HU → anagrelide / peg-IFN). Private hematologists can prescribe registered products on-label (anagrelide for thrombocythaemia) and may use peg-IFN **off-label** for ET. BESREMi ET use remains **off-label** until DoH variation. PSP terms for BESREMi were designed around **PV** — do not assume they apply to ET.

---

## Competitive / access map for an ET application

```
Low / very-low risk     →  aspirin ± observe
High-risk first line    →  HU (General, HA-funded)  or  peg-IFN (younger; often self-pay if not listed)
HU fail / intolerant    →  anagrelide (Special)  or  peg-IFN  [today’s second-line pair]
                          SURPASS-ET positions ropeg vs anagrelide in this slot
Symptoms / post-ET MF   →  ruxolitinib (SFI + CCF) — different disease stage, not ET cytoreduction
```

**Unmet need that is public-source defensible:** after HU, the funded ET-specific alternative on HADF is **anagrelide** (Special). Peg-IFN is used but is likely hepatitis-labelled and often not HA-funded for ET. There is **no interferon with an ET indication** on the Hong Kong PI today.
