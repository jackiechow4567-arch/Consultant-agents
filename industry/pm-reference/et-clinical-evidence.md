## How to brief the pathway (ET indication application)

- **Disease:** BCR-ABL-negative MPN; drivers **JAK2V617F, CALR, MPL** (or triple-negative). Risk is thrombosis, haemorrhage, and late MF/AML.
- **Risk (IPSET-thrombosis / Tefferi 2024):** very low / low / intermediate / high. Cytoreduction is indicated for **high-risk** and optional for intermediate-risk.
- **First line (today):** HU or peg-IFN-α (younger / pregnancy-planning). Anagrelide is generally **second line** after HU (PT-1; EMA labelling pattern).
- **Unmet need SURPASS addresses:** ~one-third of high-risk ET become HU-resistant or intolerant; anagrelide is the usual second line but has CV / bleeding / MF signals (PT-1, EXELS).
- **Proposed slot for ropeg (application narrative, not HK promo):** second-line high-risk ET after HU, especially with leukocytosis — **named SURPASS-ET vs anagrelide**. EXCEED-ET supports activity in treatment-naïve and across mutations — **supportive**, not a substitute for the Phase 3 population.
