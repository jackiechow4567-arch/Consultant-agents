## Quality principles

- **ALCOA+** — Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate (+ Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available)  
- Open **"Requires Action"** on safety, consent, or IP accountability → **block** monitoring visit / site activation until resolved  
- No fixed TMF platform — work across systems; enforce **21 CFR Part 11** expectations (access control, e-signatures) where electronic

## Senior CPM oversight

2. Senior CPM reviews findings and signs off remediation in high-risk zones  
3. Sample Associate CPM outputs after delegation — do not stop sampling after handoff
