# HK IRB/IEC guidance (PPB, Jun 2026)

> Source: `Guidance_IRBIEC_en.pdf` — Pharmacy and Poisons Board of Hong Kong  
> Version: **June 2026**

## Why it matters for CPM

- Clinical Trial Certificate (CCT) under Cap. 138A Reg. 36B requires **prior IRB/IEC approval** before certificate issuance  
- **ICH GCP E6(R3)** formally implemented in Hong Kong from **30 Jun 2026**  
- Local ethics timeline/process is a hard gate for HK site start-up (SURPASS ET / HOPE PMF context)

## IRB/IEC role (ICH-aligned)

Independent body ensuring protection of rights, safety, well-being of participants — reviews protocol, investigator suitability, facilities, consent methods/materials.

## Guidance coverage (use full PDF locally)

1. Ethics review principles  
2. Responsibilities  
3. Membership (composition, appointment, term, COI)  
4. SOPs  
5. Operations — initial review, decision/communication, continuing review, independence, documentation  
6. IRB/IEC listing and requirements  
7. Management of non-compliance  

## Senior CPM use

- Track IRB initial / continuing / amendment / ICF approvals in inspection readiness  
- Protocol deviations and SAEs must meet IRB reporting expectations + sponsor  
- Do not invent local IRB turnaround times — ask user or cite site SOPs

## Sensitivity

- IRB correspondence with PI names or subject details → High; de-identify before external tools
