# FDA BIMO CP 7348.810 — Sponsors & CROs (distilled)

> Source: `CP7348.810_BIMO_Sponsors-and-CROs_FINAL.pdf` / `2_FDAB-1.pdf`; summary notes in `Summary-of-CP7348.810.docx`  
> Implementation: **15 Sep 2021**. For FDA staff — Senior CPM uses as **inspector lens** for sponsor/CRO oversight.

## Purpose

1. Protect rights, safety, welfare of human subjects  
2. Verify accuracy/reliability of data submitted to FDA  
3. Assess sponsor/CRO compliance with FDA regs and GCP  

## Scope

Domestic and **foreign** inspections of sponsors, CROs, sponsor-investigators, monitors — drugs, biologics, devices, etc. “Sponsor” includes entities to whom sponsor obligations are **transferred in writing** (CRO).

## Inspection focus areas (Part III)

| Area | What inspectors evaluate |
|------|--------------------------|
| Organization & personnel | Structure, responsibilities, qualifications of staff/vendors |
| ClinicalTrials.gov | Registration and results posting where applicable |
| Selection & monitoring of CIs | How sponsor selects, trains, oversees investigators |
| Outsourced services | Written transfer of obligations; oversight of vendors |
| Monitoring procedures & activities | Monitor selection, procedures, actual monitoring quality |
| Quality assurance | QA activities supporting data integrity |
| Safety / AE reporting | Timely review and reporting of SAEs |
| DSMB/DMC | Safety oversight committees where used |
| Data collection & handling | Source → CRF → database; integrity; Part 11 systems |
| Electronic records/signatures | **21 CFR Part 11** — validation, access controls |
| Records custody & retention | Retention and availability |
| Interviews of personnel | Consistency of answers with records |
| Financial disclosure | Collection and reporting of CI financial interests |
| Investigational product | Accountability, labeling, integrity |
| International data | Foreign studies / foreign inspections (links to **21 CFR 312.120** usability) |

## Classification (agent memory)

| Classification | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| **NAI** | No Action Indicated |
| **VAI** | Voluntary Action Indicated |
| **OAI** | Official Action Indicated — escalate Fast (per role-clinical-pm: **2 hours** to RA/QA/Legal if suspected) |

## Field reporting (context for responses)

- Preliminary findings + any FDA 483 → center BIMO POC generally within **3 business days** of inspection end  
- Full EIR via eNSpect  

## Senior CPM use

- Evaluate CRO monitoring, SDV, and oversight **as if for a 7348.810 inspection**  
- Prefer documented transfer of obligations (TORO), root-cause CAPA, effectiveness checks  
- Part 11 / access control gaps = data-integrity hard constraint
