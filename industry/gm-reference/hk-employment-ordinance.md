# HK Employment Ordinance — GM cheat sheet

> Source: Labour Dept *A Concise Guide to the Employment Ordinance* (Dec 2025) + Cap. 57 consolidated text (Jan 2026). **Not legal advice.**

## Scope

- **Employment Ordinance (Cap. 57)** applies to most HK employees (exceptions: family in same dwelling, certain overseas contracts, seafarers, registered apprentices).
- **All covered employees:** wage payment, deduction limits, statutory holidays.
- **Continuous contract employees:** rest days, paid annual leave, sickness allowance, severance, long service payment, etc.

## Continuous contract (from 18 Jan 2026)

Employee on continuous contract if:

1. Employed **4+ weeks** by same employer, **and**
2. Meets hours test: **≥17 hours/week** each week, **or** (if any week <17h) **≥68 hours** in that week + prior 3 weeks.

> Pre–18 Jan 2026: 18+ hours/week for 4 weeks.

**Burden of proof:** employer must prove contract is *not* continuous if disputed.

## Written contract essentials (Ch. 2)

Before employment, employer must inform employee clearly of:

1. **Wages** (rate, OT, allowances; piece/hour/day/week basis)
2. **Wage period**
3. **Notice** to terminate
4. **End-of-year payment** (if any) — amount/proportion and period

- Written contract → give employee a copy.
- Oral contract → provide written particulars if employee requests in writing.
- **Any term reducing EO rights is void.**

**Default:** continuous contract without express term = **month-to-month**, renewable.

**Records:** keep 12 months wage/employment history per employee (identity, job title, wages, hours if applicable, leave taken/paid, termination date, etc.).

## GM-relevant chapters (quick map)

| Topic | EO chapter | GM action |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Wages / deductions | Ch. 3 | Pay on time; no unlawful deductions |
| Rest days, holidays, annual leave | Ch. 4 | Schedule + pay statutory entitlements |
| Sickness / maternity / paternity | Ch. 5–7 | Policy aligned to statutory minima |
| End of year payment | Ch.  8 | Define in contract if applicable |
| Termination / notice | Ch. 9 | Follow contract or EO defaults |
| Unfair dismissal protection | Ch. 10 | Document lawful reasons |
| Severance / long service | Ch. 11 | Budget on restructuring |
| Anti-union discrimination | Ch. 12 | HR policy check |
| MPF offset abolition | Appendix 2 | **No MPF offset against SP/LSP** (from 1 May 2025 transition rules in guide) |

## Termination (GM checklist)

1. Check **written contract notice** vs EO minimums.
2. Determine if **summary dismissal** grounds exist (serious misconduct — see EO Ch. 9).
3. Calculate **notice pay**, **annual leave balance**, **severance/LSP** if applicable.
4. Complete **written records** and exit documentation.
5. **Labour Tribunal / MECAB** exposure if unpaid awards (Ch. 13 employer criminal liability).

## PharmaEssentia HK subsidiary hooks

- Small HK office → every hire may trigger **continuous contract** quickly; part-time/agency rules differ.
- **Associate CPM / PM** roles: clarify hours, OT, and rest days in contract — avoid accidental continuous-contract surprises for contractors.
- Restructuring or role elimination → severance/LSP and **MPF** calculations need HR + counsel, not GM alone.
- GM **cannot sign all contracts** per role-gm — route employment contracts through authorized signatory + HR.

## When to escalate

- Disputed continuous contract status
- Summary dismissal of senior employee
- Collective redundancy / mass termination
- Any Labour Tribunal claim or union-related issue
