# Creativity & systems thinking — Global Immersion (Nathan Crilly)

> Sources: Session 4.1 Creativity in business; Session 4.2 Ways of thinking — 11 Jun 2026.

## Three thinking modes (under pressure)

| Mode | Focus | GM use |
|------|-------|--------|
| **Critical** | Clarity, evidence, disciplined judgment | Formulary dossier, P&L, compliance reviews |
| **Creative** | Exploration, imagination, possibilities | PSP design, channel mix, innovation options |
| **Systems** | Structure, interconnections, long-term effects | HK healthcare ecosystem, HQ-subsidiary feedback loops |

**No profile is “right”** — overusing one mode creates blind spots. Invite missing modes from team.

## Creative individual / team / leader / org / space

| Level | Enablers |
|-------|----------|
| **Individual** | Curiosity, tolerance of ambiguity, cross-domain connections |
| **Team** | Diversity, autonomy, shared vision, **psychological safety** |
| **Leader** | Support autonomy; model creative process; assess originality + viability; manage implementation risk |
| **Organization** | Top-level support, meaningful work, incentives, time/money/people for creativity |
| **Space** | Views, ceiling height, solo + group space, food/drink access |

**Cross-cultural note (Engelen et al.):** transformational leadership → innovation orientation; only **“providing an appropriate model”** and **“accepting group goals”** are culturally stable — adapt other behaviors to HK/APAC context.

## Systems thinking components

1. **Holistic view** — whole system, not isolated parts  
2. **Interconnections** — how parts influence each other  
3. **Feedback loops** — influence runs both ways  
4. **Leverage points** — where small change → big impact  
5. **Sustainability over time** — flexibility, adaptability, scalability  

## Systems mapping (6 steps)

1. Define **system boundary** (in/out)  
2. Identify **nodes** (people, machines, rules, documents…)  
3. Draw **edges** (causation, influence, dependency…)  
4. Map **flows** (people, money, data, regulation…)  
5. Highlight **feedback loops**  
6. Identify **leverage points**  

**HK pharma example nodes:** patient, HA formulary committee, private hematologist, HQ medical, PV, distributor, PSP nurse, regulator.

## GM prompts

- Under deadline, which thinking mode do you **forget**? (Often systems → restructure risk)
- Do PM (creative/commercial) and CPM (critical/compliance) profiles **complement** or clash?
- For innovation bets (6501W lens): does org provide **time and cover** for experiments?
