> Source: `Session-1.4-Zoe-Arden-080626.pdf` — MBA Global Immersion, 8 Jun 2026.

## Core definition

> **Leadership is accepting responsibility to create the conditions for collective movement towards a shared meaningful goal.**  
> (Rost, Ganz — via session)

## Mental models

- We default to **simple stories** and self-limiting habits (Senge).
- Thriving in disruption requires **capability, confidence, courage, commitment** to lead change.
- Leadership starts with **noticing** how you show up — self-awareness + systems awareness.

## Five leadership capabilities (21st century / CISL)

| Capability | GM application |
|------------|----------------|
| **Purpose & performance** | Link HK P&L + formulary strategy to **why** subsidiary exists |
| **Collective** | Balance collaboration vs competition; purposeful alliances across HQ, HA, KOLs |
| **Place** | HK public/private split, regulatory context — decisions grounded in **local place** |
| **Creative** | Conditions for innovation (digital health bets) without breaking compliance |
| **Connected** | Interdependence: clinical, commercial, regulatory as one system |
| **Courageous** | Hard calls: say no to HQ, stop non-compliant promo, escalate access blockers |

## Who / how / why / where framework

| Lens | Question |
|------|----------|
| **Who** | Leadership as position and person |
| **How** | Leadership as process |
| **Why** | Purpose and performance |
| **Where** | Leadership in place (time, space, market) |

## Levers of influence (for GM)

- Power of **position**, **expertise**, **purpose**, **networks**, **intuition**, **legacy**
- Also: truth, passion, role modelling, **regulation**, money, **story**

## Personal boardroom

- Who sits at your **personal boardroom table**? (mentors, critics, stakeholders)
- Avoid **echo chamber** vs **eco chamber** — widen who you listen to before HQ updates.

## Listening shift

| From | To |
|------|-----|
| Listen to fix / win | Listen to learn / see |
| Make problem go away | Make problem clearer |

## Career hooks (GM)

- **HQ escalation:** use collective + connected leadership — name allies and levers before asking HQ for budget.
- **Formulary / access:** place-based leadership — HA system is the “where.”
- **Team health:** courageous leadership on compliance non-negotiables.
