# HK employment contracts — GM checklist

> Sources: Labour Dept *Sample Employment Contract* (4/2024), *Written Contract of Employment* guide, EO Concise Guide Ch. 2.

## Why this matters for GM

HK subsidiary hires (associates, admin, local clinical support) need **written clarity** before start. GM approves headcount; HR/Legal executes contract — GM should know **minimum clauses** and **cannot** rely on informal offers.

## Sample contract clause checklist

Use Labour Dept sample (`ECsample.pdf`) as structure. Each clause needs explicit tick/values:

| # | Clause | GM review point |
|---|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | Commencement | Fixed term vs open-ended; start date |
| 2 | Probation | Yes/no; duration (typical 3–6 months in handbook templates) |
| 3 | Position / section | Job title matches approved JD |
| 4 | Place of work | HK office / hybrid / travel |
| 5 | Working hours | Fixed vs shift; total hours; EO compliance |
| 6 | Meal break | Paid/unpaid; counts as working time? |
| 7 | Rest days | ≥1 in every 7 days (EO) |
| 8 | Wages | Basic rate + allowances; payment frequency |
| 9 | Overtime | OT pay vs time-off in lieu |
| 10 | Holidays | Statutory + general holidays |
| 11 | Paid annual leave | EO minimum or better |
| 12 | Maternity / paternity | Statutory entitlements |
| 13 | Sick leave | Sickness allowance rules |
| 14 | End of year payment | If offered — define clearly |
| 15 | Notice period | Termination by either party |
| 16 | MPF | Scheme enrollment |
| 17 | Confidentiality / IP | Especially for pharma roles |
| 18 | Governing law | Hong Kong |

## Information employer must give (even if oral hire)

Wages, wage period, notice, end-of-year payment (if any) — **before** employment begins.

## Written contract guide themes (`WrittenContract.pdf`)

- Encourage **written** contracts for clarity and dispute reduction.
- Terms cannot **reduce** EO rights.
- On change of conditions → inform employee; provide written copy of amendments on request.
- Reference EO Concise Guide for notice periods when contract silent.

## GM Do / Don't

**Do:** Ensure offer letter + contract align with approved comp band and HQ headcount approval.  
**Do:** Flag **probation + PIP** linkage before confirming hire.  
**Don't:** Promise benefits not in contract or handbook.  
**Don't:** Sign employment contracts if role-gm limits signing authority — route to MD/HR.
