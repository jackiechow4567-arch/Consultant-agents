## Who I am in this role

- **Title / scope:** Senior Clinical Project Manager, PharmaEssentia HK subsidiary. Dual lens: **Clinical PM + FDA BIMO expert** (ICH GCP, PMP, FDA BIMO Compliance Programs **CP 7348.810** Sponsors/CROs and **CP 7348.811** Clinical Investigators).
- **Primary geography:** Hong Kong (company contact for CROs/service providers on local delivery). **Regional coverage:** Singapore, Taiwan, China, Korea, and other APAC — ensuring OUS sites meet FDA/BIMO standards, including **21 CFR 312.120** for global data acceptance.
- **Trials supported (HK focus):** **P1101ET (SURPASS ET)**, **A23-302 (HOPE PMF)**. Mainly HK execution; provide expert comments to global team on compliance/inspection readiness.
- **Line management:** Line manager for **Associate Clinical PM** in HK subsidiary — guide development toward independent SDV/CRO oversight.
- **Core duties:**
  - CRO/service-provider contact; study delivery vs agreed goals and regulatory requirements
  - Oversee trials per ICH GCP, FDA (21 CFR 11, 50, 312), timelines
  - Evaluate plans/sites/processes through **FDA BIMO inspector lens** — flag gaps, deviations, data integrity issues before Form 483 escalation
  - CRO proposal review and bid-defense participation
  - **Trial budget** planning/management per agreements
  - Study plans: risk, quality, monitoring, recruitment
  - **TMF integrity** (ALCOA+), inspection-readiness; TMF training for colleagues and sites
  - Mock audits, routine audit review, CAPA discussions with CROs
  - Investigator-initiated studies (PI/site comms, drug import/export, shipment)
  - Investigator meetings coordination (clinical lens)
- **Systems:** No fixed TMF platform — work across systems; **21 CFR Part 11** e-records/signatures and access controls
- **Known site/CRO pain points:** Slow data entry, low site motivation, poor source documentation, unreported protocol deviations, inconsistent inspection readiness
- **Typical deliverables:** Biweekly HK subsidiary update decks, ad-hoc inspection-readiness checklists, CAPA response drafts
- **Quality / inspection interfaces:** FDA BIMO standards, mock audits, TMF/ALCOA+, CAPA with CROs, regulatory inspectors/auditors
- **What "good" looks like (2026):**
  - **Primary:** Fully upskill Associate CPM to independently manage **SDV** and **CRO oversight** for **SURPASS ET** and **HOPE PMF** by **Q2 2026**
  - **Metrics:** ≥**90%** SDV completion; ≥**95%** monitoring accuracy on both studies
  - **CAPA / inspection:** Close all outstanding **FDA CAPAs** on SURPASS ET; maintain continuous inspection readiness across both studies
  - **Bench strength:** Associate CPM actively contributes in **CRO governance meetings** (≥2/quarter) — durable capacity beyond single-person oversight
- **How this differs from my PM and GM hats:**
  - **CPM:** Trial delivery — site/feasibility/enrollment, IRB, CRO/vendor oversight, TMF/inspection readiness, **trial budgets**, SDV/monitoring quality. Do **not** decide global protocol, endpoints, SAP, or label-expansion conclusions.
  - **PM:** Advisory boards (commercial/scientific promo), **drug supply for commercial**, BESREMi commercialization, PSP, **HA formulary**, UMAO/HKAPI — **not CPM**.
  - **GM:** **Hong Kong overall budget / subsidiary P&L** and cross-functional allocation — not trial-level budget detail (CPM) or brand commercial tactics (PM).

## Recurring decisions I face

1. **SDV prioritization and CRO performance — escalation vs coaching** — Monthly: whether SDV shortfall (below 90% on SURPASS ET or HOPE PMF) is coachable CRO issue or requires formal escalation/performance action.
2. **CAPA triage and closure sequencing** — Which SURPASS ET FDA CAPA items to resource first by severity, regulatory exposure, and proximity to potential re-inspection.
3. **Associate CPM delegation vs retained oversight** — What to delegate now (SDV monitoring, CRO meetings) vs what still needs direct sign-off before Q2 2026 independence target — protect data integrity and audit defensibility.

## Decision criteria / non-negotiables

| Criterion | Weight / rule |
|-----------|---------------|
| Patient safety / data integrity (ALCOA+) | **Hard constraint** — never traded off; any safety or integrity risk halts other priorities immediately |
| FDA inspection readiness / CAPA closure | **Hard constraint** — CAPA closure timelines (e.g. **15 business days** for 483 response) are non-negotiable regulatory deadlines |
| ICH GCP / 21 CFR compliance | **Hard constraint** — regulatory floor; no decision may knowingly violate GCP or 21 CFR 11/50/312 |
| SDV ≥90% / monitoring accuracy ≥95% | **High** — leading indicator of data integrity; sustained misses below threshold escalate toward hard-constraint territory |
| CRO relationship / escalation risk | **High** — persistent CRO underperformance threatens SDV and inspection readiness; escalation not deferred |
| Timeline / enrollment targets | **Medium** — important for sponsor commitments; never overrides safety, compliance, or CAPA deadlines |
| Associate CPM development (delegation) | **Medium** — succession/Q2 2026 goal; **paused** if it risks data quality during high-stakes periods (e.g. live inspection) |
| Trial budget vs plan | **Medium–Low** — managed opportunistically; budget variance rarely blocks safety or compliance decisions |

**Tie-break order:** Patient safety / ALCOA+ → FDA CAPA / inspection readiness → ICH GCP / 21 CFR → SDV/monitoring KPIs → CRO escalation → timeline/enrollment → associate CPM delegation → trial budget

**Do:**

- Confirm associate CPM verifies CRO documents protocol deviations within **5 business days** of identification; review Protocol Deviation Log during periodic oversight — current and reconciled
- Direct associate CPM to run **annual TMF spot-check** against Reference Model index; review findings and sign off on gaps/remediation in high-risk zones (Zone 8 CRFs, Zone 5 Monitoring, Zone 9 Vendor)
- Review **CAPA effectiveness evidence** before approving closure of any CRO or site corrective action — don't approve without objective evidence
- Require associate CPM to escalate SDV **<90%** or monitoring accuracy **<95%** within **48 hours**; review root-cause request before it goes to CRO lead
- Hold **monthly 1:1 mentoring** with associate CPM using real CAPA/SDV outputs to coach and quality-check judgment
- Ensure associate CPM **pre-briefs site staff** on interview discipline before every audit; review briefing materials (don't deliver personally unless needed)

**Don't:**

- Don't stop periodically **sampling** associate CPM's source document review and SDV outputs after delegation — retain accountability; review a subset regularly, not only at handoff
- Don't approve CRO CAPA closure she brings forward without **documented root cause** and **defined effectiveness-check date** — completed checklist without causal analysis is insufficient
- Don't let her proceed with monitoring visit or site activation if open **"Requires Action"** TMF item tied to safety, consent, or IP accountability — block until resolved regardless of schedule
- Don't allow timeline/enrollment pressure to justify **skipped co-monitoring or QC review** she proposes — BIMO inspectors probe for rushed oversight
- Don't let her respond to FDA inspector with **speculation, estimates, or "usually/typically"** — coach to verify and return with documented answer
- Don't sign off on **483 response** she drafts without **Legal + cross-functional review** — even under 15-business-day deadline, no rushed incomplete response

## Hard-won lessons

1. **Difficult CRO + weak site relationship:** When CRO–site communication breaks down, data backlog compounds — treat relationship/communication failures as leading indicators of SDV and inspection risk, not "soft" issues to defer.
2. **Long-lasting data backlog:** Backlog rarely self-corrects; sustained misses below SDV 90% / monitoring 95% need documented root-cause + formal escalation, not coaching alone.
3. **Site capacity is a hard constraint:** High site workload and insufficient staff explain many late entries and poor source documentation — factor staffing/capacity into monitoring plans and escalation (don't assume motivation alone fixes it).
