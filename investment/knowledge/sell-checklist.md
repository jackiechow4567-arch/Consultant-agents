# Sell checklist

Two books: **lock profit** and **control risk**. Inspired by the user's Train-the-Trader sell guideline. Research support only.

The Bot recommends; the user executes.

## Lock-profit sells

- [ ] Move ≥ **1×** the original stop distance → discuss a first trim
- [ ] Move has reached the user’s **average win** → discuss a larger trim
- [ ] Move has reached **2×** that average win → be more aggressive taking money off
- [ ] **+15–20%** from a proper buy → sell **20–33%** (sometimes **50%**); do not try to nail the high
- [ ] Weakening signals at highs (exhaustion gap, climactic run) → sell strength
- [ ] Name is still the strongest holding → keep a runner; do not dump the whole winner just because it is up

Raise the stop toward breakeven as soon as the trade allows. Never let a fat winner become a loser.

## Risk-control sells (do not debate)

- [ ] Price **hits the stop**
- [ ] Drawdown **>8%** from the buy
- [ ] Sell alert / “wrong behaviour” / egg-style reversal
- [ ] Tape itself is rolling over
- [ ] Using margin **and** the tape or the name looks hostile → flatten risk
- [ ] Tape has entered grind / 血汗錢
- [ ] Fast, heavy-volume break of the 20-day or 50-day soon after entry
- [ ] Largest drop in months **on heavy volume**

## Other sell triggers from the mindset list

- Abnormal action → sell first
- Oversized into earnings → cut to a small stub **before** the print
- Late-stage market melt-up → sell partials, raise cash, no new leverage language
- Working trade → consider the **next add**; failing trade → cut size, do not add

Never average down. Adding is only for names that are already working, at the next valid buy point, in breakout order — not by personal favourite.
