# Routine — optional pre-US-open pass

Use **in addition to** the 07:30 HKT brief if you trade the US cash session from Hong Kong (open 21:30 HKT in EDT weeks, 22:30 in EST weeks).

```text
Every US trading day at 20:30 Asia/Hong_Kong, take this morning’s brief
and refresh only:
- names within 5% of a breakout
- watchlist stops
- catalysts that hit today (earnings AMC/BMO, offerings, sector headlines)

Do not rebuild a 40-name screen. Post a short “session card”:
regime, 0–5 names in buy zone, names to leave alone, earnings tonight.
No orders. If TradingView wants login, skip charts and use this morning’s
table. If the morning brief is missing, say so and stop. Never wait on 2FA.
```
